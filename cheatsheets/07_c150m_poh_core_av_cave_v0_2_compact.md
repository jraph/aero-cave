#### Manoeuvre / structural limits

- Utility category. Approved only: chandelles/lazy eights/steep turns `95 kt`; spins and non-whip stalls by slow deceleration.
- Load factors: flaps UP `+4.4/-1.76 g`; DOWN `+3.5 g`; no abrupt controls above `Va`.

- `T:` approval still requires training, altitude, area, W&B and regulatory compliance.

#### Oil

- Sump `6 qt`; system `7 qt` with full-flow filter; minimum `4 qt`; POH suggests `5 qt` for flights `<3 h`, `6 qt` extended.
- Break-in: straight mineral until POH hours/consumption stabilized, then ashless dispersant.
- Viscosity: `SAE 40` above `4°C/40°F`; `10W30 or SAE 20` below.

#### Preflight — POH highlights

- Remove all frost/ice/snow; controls internally free of ice/debris.
- Cabin: lock out; ignition OFF; master ON gauges check/OFF; fuel ON.
- Wings: tie-down/tire; sump before first flight and after refuel; visually verify fuel/caps.
- Nose: oil (`4 qt` min; `6` extended), strainer drain≈`4 sec`, prop/spinner, filter, light, strut/tire, static port.
- `T:` any water→continue draining all specified points until clear.
***
#### Before start

- `P:` preflight complete→seats/belts/shoulder harnesses adjusted/locked→fuel ON→radios/electrical OFF→brakes test/set→breakers IN.
***
#### Start

- `P:` MIX RICH→carb heat COLD→master ON→prime→throttle `1/4 in`→CLEAR→START→oil P.
- Prime: warm `1–2`, cold up to `6`; flooded: MIX ICO/throttle full/crank/restart no prime.
- `!:` oil P within `30 sec` summer/`60 sec` very cold or stop. Carb heat only if needed; air unfiltered.
***
#### Taxi

- Minimum speed/braking; use wind controls.
- Carb heat COLD unless necessary; heated induction air is unfiltered.
- Low RPM over gravel/cinders to protect propeller.
***
#### Before takeoff

- `P:` doors→brake→controls→instruments→fuel ON→MIX RICH below `5000 ft`→trim→`1700 RPM`.
- Run-up: each mag drop `≤150 RPM`, difference `≤75 RPM`; carb-heat drop; engine/ammeter/suction; radios/lights/friction.
- Field `>5000 ft`: full-throttle static lean for max RPM.
***
#### Normal takeoff

- `P:` flaps `0°`→carb heat COLD→full throttle→lift nosewheel at `50 KIAS`→climb `60–70`.
- Verify expected static/full-throttle RPM and engine indications before commitment.
***
#### Short-field takeoff

- `P:` flaps `0°`→carb heat COLD→brakes APPLY→full throttle→brakes RELEASE→slightly tail-low→`60 KIAS` with obstacle.
- POH obstacle-clearance speed is `60`, despite pure `Vx=56`; it accounts for near-ground turbulence/overall obstacle performance.
***
#### Soft / rough field flap logic

- Normal/short: flaps UP. `10°` cuts ground run≈`10%`, advantage gone by `50 ft`; reserve for minimum run/soft-rough field.
- With obstacle, normally retain `10°` until clear; hot/high marginal climb may require retraction. `>10°` prohibited for TKOF.
***
#### Crosswind takeoff

- Minimum flap needed; accelerate slightly above normal, lift off decisively, then coordinate into wind.
- `T:` school technique may refine POH baseline.
***
#### Climb

- Normal `65–75 KIAS`, flaps UP/full throttle; `Vy 68 SL→62 @10,000`; `Vx 56`.
- MIX full rich below `5000 ft`, may lean above for smoothness; keep steep low-speed climbs brief for cooling.
***
#### Cruise / leaning

- `P:` `2000–2750 RPM`, ≤`75%`→trim→LEAN; normal cruise `55–75%`, charts assume recommended lean.
- `T:` RPM alone≠power across altitude/temp; use chart/power computer.
***
#### Before landing

- `P:` seats/belts/harnesses secure→mixture RICH→full carb heat before closing throttle.
***
#### Normal landing

- `P:` `60–70` UP→flaps below `85`→`50–60` DOWN→power-off mains first→nose gently→minimum brake.
***
#### Short-field landing

- `P:` flaps `40°` below `85`→`52` smooth-air→power path→idle after obstacle→mains→nose down→full-up elevator/max non-skid brake→flaps UP.
- Add slight speed in turbulence. `T:` excess speed sharply increases distance.
***
#### Crosswind landing

- Crab, wing-low or combination; strong XW: minimum flap needed for field. Maintain directional/wind control after touchdown.
***
#### Balked landing

- `P:` throttle FULL→carb heat COLD→flaps RETRACT TO `20°`→`55 KIAS`→flaps retract slowly after safe climb.
- `T:` abrupt full-flap retraction at low speed can cause sink.
***
#### After landing / secure

- After landing: flaps UP; carb heat COLD.
- Secure: parking brake SET→radios/electrical OFF→mixture ICO→ignition OFF→master OFF→control lock.
***

***
#### Engine failure — takeoff roll

- `P:` throttle IDLE→brakes APPLY→flaps RETRACT→mixture ICO→ignition OFF→master OFF.
***
#### Engine failure — immediately after takeoff

- `P:` `60 KIAS`→mixture ICO→fuel OFF→ignition OFF→flaps as required→master OFF.
- `!:` speed first; landing path ahead/near-ahead.
***
#### Engine failure — in flight / restart

- `P:` `60 KIAS`→carb heat ON→primer IN/LOCKED→fuel ON→mixture RICH→ignition BOTH, or START if prop stopped.
- If no restart: commit to forced landing checklist.
***
#### Forced landing without power

- `P:` `65 UP / 55 DOWN`→mixture ICO→fuel OFF→ignition OFF→flaps as required, `40°` recommended→master OFF→doors UNLATCH before touchdown→slightly tail-low→brakes heavily.
- `T:` only use flap after landing is assured.
***
#### Precautionary landing with power

- `P:` `60`, flaps `20°`→inspect selected field→retract flap after safe climb→radio/electrical OFF→flaps `40°` final→`55`→master OFF→doors unlatch→slightly tail-low→ignition OFF→heavy brakes.
***
#### Ditching

- `P:` MAYDAY `121.5` + position/intentions→secure/jettison heavy items→high wind/heavy sea INTO wind; light wind/heavy swells PARALLEL swells→flaps `40°`→power for `300 fpm @55`→doors unlatch→level attitude at touchdown→protect face→evacuate→inflate vests/raft after exit.
***
#### Engine fire during start

- Continue cranking to draw fire into engine.
- If engine starts: `1700 RPM` briefly→shutdown→inspect.
- If no start: continue cranking→obtain extinguisher→master OFF→ignition OFF→fuel OFF→extinguish→inspect/repair.
***
#### Engine fire in flight

- `P:` mixture ICO→fuel OFF→master OFF→cabin heat/air OFF except wing-root vents→`85 KIAS`; if fire persists, increase speed to find non-combustible mixture→forced landing.
- Do not restart.
***
#### Electrical fire

- `P:` master OFF→all other switches except ignition OFF→vents/cabin air/heat CLOSED→extinguisher.
- After extinguisher: ventilate cabin.
- If power essential and fire fully out: master ON→breakers inspect; do not reset faulty breaker→switches ON one at a time with delay.
***
#### Cabin / wing fire

- Cabin: master OFF→vents/heat CLOSED→extinguisher→land ASAP.
- Wing: nav/strobe/pitot heat OFF→side-slip to keep flames from fuel tank/cabin→land ASAP with flaps retracted.
***
#### Inadvertent icing

- `L:` known icing prohibited.
- `P:` pitot heat ON if installed→turn back/change altitude to less-conducive temperature→max defrost heat→increase engine RPM→watch carb/filter ice; carb heat as required→lean for max RPM if continuous heat→nearest airport.
- `T:` this is an escape checklist, not authorization to remain in icing.
***
#### Spin recovery

- `P:` throttle IDLE + ailerons NEUTRAL→full opposite rudder→immediately/briskly move wheel forward enough to break stall→hold until rotation stops→neutralize rudder→smooth dive recovery.
- Training: POH recommends `1–2` turns; cabin clear/loose items secured; no baggage or occupied child seat.
- `L:` intentional spins with flaps extended prohibited.
***
#### Landing without elevator control

- Trim level at approx `55 KIAS`, flaps `20°`.
- Do not change trim; use power exclusively for glide path.
- At flare: full nose-up trim + power to rotate to level touchdown attitude; close throttle at touchdown.
***
#### Vacuum-system failure / cloud

- Directional and attitude indicators may be disabled; electrically powered turn coordinator/turn-and-bank remains primary legacy backup.
- POH provides timed standard-rate 180° and emergency-descent procedures.
- `T:` actual installed instruments/avionics may differ; use current supplement/SOP and declare early.
***
#### Spin vs spiral memory

- Spin: stalled, low AS, rotation.
- Spiral: AS increasing; recovery = throttle idle→wings level→smooth pullout.
***
#### Electrical system

- `14 V DC`, `60 A` alternator, `12 V/25 Ah` battery, single bus; split master `BAT`=aircraft power, `ALT`=field.
- Mags independent of master/battery; ammeter shows battery charge/discharge.
- `T:` prolonged ALT OFF may deplete battery enough to prevent alternator restart.
***
#### High-voltage light / alternator

- Over-voltage sensor removes field current and shuts alternator; red `HIGH VOLTAGE` light illuminates.
- `P:` master OFF then ON once to reset.
- If light stays out: charging resumed.
- If light returns: malfunction; terminate flight as soon as practical.
- `T:` exact current-aircraft checklist/supplement wins.
***
#### Carburetor / induction

- Updraft float carburetor, fixed jet, accelerator pump, manual mixture, idle cut-off.
- Fuel reaches carb by gravity.
- Full carb heat at full throttle causes about `200–250 RPM` loss.
- Primer draws from fuel strainer and injects intake manifold; lock primer IN.
***
#### Cooling

- Ram-air cooling; no manual cowl-flap control.
- Winterization kit intended for consistently below `-7°C / 20°F`.
***
#### Brakes

- Single-disc hydraulic brake on each main wheel; interconnected toe brakes; parking brake.
- If braking fades: release then reapply firmly.
- Spongy/excess travel: pumping may restore pressure.
- One brake weak/failed: use other sparingly + opposite rudder.
