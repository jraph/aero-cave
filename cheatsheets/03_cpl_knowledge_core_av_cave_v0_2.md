#### CPL-A requirements

- Age `18`; valid Cat 1 medical for CPL; `80h` CPL ground school; CPAER `60%` each subject + overall.
- Experience: `200h` aeroplane TT incl `100 PIC` + `20 PIC XC`.
- Post-PPL training: `65h` = `35 dual + 30 solo VFR`.
- Dual: `5 night incl 2 XC` + `5 XC incl night-XC credit` + `20 instrument` max `10 sim/FTD`.
- Solo: `25 h skill improvement` including XC of at least `300 NM total`, full-stop landings at `3 different aerodromes other than departure`, one at least `250 NM straight-line from departure`; plus `5 h solo night` including `10 takeoffs, circuits and landings`.
- Current non-integrated rule effective `2025-12-17`: special Newfoundland alternative uses `150 NM` straight-line, with the same 300 NM total/3 full stops. Older 300 NM radius wording is not the current 421.30 rule. Integrated-course requirements must be checked separately. [STD 421.30(4)](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-421-flight-crew-permits-licences-ratings-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars)
- Skill: flight test within `12mo` before application. `T:` daylight restriction if night req not met.

#### Flight test / standard

- Admission: ID + licence + CPL medical proof + instructor letter ≤`30d` + ≥`75%` flying exp + written proof/deficiency review if applicable.
- Stable VFR app: correct path/config/power/checklist/brief; ROD≤`1000 fpm`; speed `+10/-5`; small corrections; stable by `200 AGL` or overshoot.
- `CONTEXT:` CPL flight-test evaluation criterion; not a CAR operating minimum and not the IFR stable-approach gate.
- `T:` examiner wants safe PIC judgement, not rote manoeuvre.

#### CPL mindset

- PPL asks “can I fly?” CPL asks “should I accept/continue as paid PIC?”
- `T:` legal≠professional. customer/time/weather/fuel/runway pressure = threat multipliers.
- ADM: PAVE before start; 5P enroute; DECIDE abnormal.

- CPL privileges depend on rating/medical/recency/aircraft/category/class/ops rules.
- `T:` CPL≠703/704/705 authorization by itself. Hire/reward usually triggers operator/OC/ops rules unless exception.

#### Planning / fuel / go-no-go

- Plan: wx+NOTAM+CFS+fuel+W&B+perf+alternates/options+daylight/night+terrain/airspace.
- Fuel: taxi/start + trip + contingency + reserve + alternate/diversion + unusable.
- `T:` POH burn≠actual burn. marginal VFR + commercial pressure + unfamiliar terrain = trap.
***
#### Performance / W&B

- Calculate, don’t guess. TO/LDG: weight/temp/pressure/elev/wind/slope/surface/config/technique.
- `M:` hot-high-heavy-humid-tailwind-soft-uphill TO = bad stack.
- LDG energy: KE∝V²; +10% speed≈+21% energy. Tailwind small value, large distance penalty.
- CG=`ΣM/ΣW`; check takeoff+landing+zero-fuel if near edge. Aft CG = recovery worse; forward CG = flare authority lower.
***
#### Handling / landings

- Steep turn: clearing→entry speed/pwr→bank→back pressure→scan→rollout lead. `T:` altitude by attitude first.
- Slow flight: coord critical. Stall: AoA↓ first, power as req, coord. Spiral: idle→wings level→ease.
- Precision/P180: key positions + wind + energy. `T:` high fixable, low usually not.
- Short LDG: stable speed/profile/touchdown zone; fast final eats runway. Soft LDG: protect nose, keep moving, yoke back.
***
#### Nav / MET / systems

- ETA uses actual GS. `1-in-60:` track error≈off×60/distance flown; closing angle≈off×60/distance remaining; destination correction=error+closing angle. Double-track-error is a separate intercept technique, not a universal destination correction.
- Divert early→MTRK/dist/GS/ETA/fuel→terrain/airspace/wx→advise.
- MET trends > snapshot. Cold front=sudden/showers/TS/gusts; warm=layer/low ceiling/steady precip.
- Systems: fuel/oil/electrical/ignition/vacuum/pitot-static/flaps/brakes/heat/vent. `T:` checklist item without system model = weak oral.
***
#### Emerg / human

- Forced: control→field→plan→cause→MAYDAY→pax→secure→land. EFATO: nose/Vg→land ahead-ish.
- Declare early. Fire: POH memory items + land ASAP. CO: heat off/vent/fresh air/land.
- Haz attitudes: anti-authority/impulsivity/invulnerability/macho/resignation. Antidotes: follow rules/not so fast/could happen/chances foolish/not helpless.
