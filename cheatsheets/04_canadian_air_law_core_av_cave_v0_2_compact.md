#### PIC responsibility / emergency

- PIC is responsible; reckless/negligent operation is prohibited. `[CAR 602.01.1–602.01.2]`
- Emergency deviation only as necessary for safety. `[CAR 602.01.3]` `M:` authority≠absence of accountability.
***
#### Fitness / alcohol / drugs

- No crew duty when unfit; no impairing drugs; no alcohol within `12 h` or while affected. `[CAR 602.02–602.03]`
- `T:` legal medication≠fit to fly; self-ground if performance may be impaired.
***
#### Licence validity stack

- Required: licence/permit + ratings + medical + recency + aircraft class/type + operation authority.
- `M:` `LICENCE-RATING-MEDICAL-RECENCY-AIRCRAFT-OPERATION`; PPL/CPL alone is insufficient.
***
#### Medical category / validity — aeroplane memory

| Privilege | Medical | Typical validity |
|---|---|---|
| PPL | Category `3` or valid Cat `1` | `<40`: `60 mo`; `40+`: `24 mo` |
| CPL hire/reward | Category `1` | `12 mo`; reduced to `6 mo` if age `≥40` AND single-pilot with passengers, OR age `≥60` |

- Medical validity is calculated from the first day of the month following the medical examination/declaration. `[CAR 404.04]`
- CPL holder may exercise PPL privileges while within the applicable PPL medical-validity period. `[CAR 401.30; STD 421.30]`
- `T:` licence document does not expire like medical privileges do.
***
#### Recency

| Privilege | Requirement |
|---|---|
| general `5 y` | PIC/co-pilot in preceding `5 years`, or prescribed review + exam `[CAR 401.05(1)]` |
| recurrent `24 mo` | approved recurrent activity, recorded `[CAR 401.05(2); STD 421.05]` |
| passengers `6 mo` | `5 TO + 5 LDG`, same category/class; night flight requires all `5+5` at night `[CAR 401.05(2)(b)]` |
| instrument | qualifying test/check within `24 mo`; following first day of seventh month after test/check: `6 approaches in preceding 6 mo` to specified minima; no separate `6 h` requirement; retain records `3 y` [CAR 401.05(3)–(3.2)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-401.05.html) |

- `T:` medical, general, passenger and instrument recency are separate clocks; solo legality≠passenger legality.
***
#### Instrument-rating recency

- `24 months:` qualifying instrument flight test, IPC or other accepted test/check under CAR 401.05(3).
- `Six approaches / six months:` following the first day of the seventh month after that test/check, complete six instrument approaches within the six months before the flight, in accordance with the procedure's specified minima.
- Aircraft: actual or simulated IMC. Other options: Level B/C/D full-flight simulator of the same instrument-rating group, or an FTD under a person qualified under Standard 425.21(9).
- `T:` no separate six-hour instrument-time requirement. The old 12-month grace period and `6–6–6` memory rule are outdated. Retain recency records for three years.
- `M:` **24-month check; after six months, six approaches in six months.** [CAR 401.05(3)–(3.2)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-401.05.html)
***
#### PPL / CPL / commercial boundary

- `PPL-A:` PIC/co-pilot only for held ratings; generally no hire/reward except CAR 401.28 cases. `[CAR 401.26; 401.28(2)–(4)]`
- Cost-share: owner/operator + non-commercial purpose + incidental passengers + reimbursement only for fuel/oil/aircraft fees. Employer/volunteer reimbursement has separate strict conditions.
- `CPL-A:` PPL/VFR-OTT privileges; PIC single-pilot rated aeroplane or co-pilot in commercial service; instruction needs instructor qualification. Day-only restriction applies if issued without night requirement. `[CAR 401.30]`
- Air transport and listed aerial work generally require an authorizing AOC. `[CAR 700.02]`
- `M:` licence qualifies pilot; AOC authorizes service. `T:` equal cost split alone is insufficient.
***
#### Aircraft operating limitations

- Operate according to AFM/POH/authorized documents, limitations, markings and placards. `[CAR 602.07; 605.04–605.05]`
- `T:` a school checklist cannot authorize something prohibited by the POH/placard.
***
#### Aircraft icing / clean aircraft

- No takeoff with frost, ice or snow adhering to critical surfaces. `[CAR 602.11]`
- When contamination may reasonably be expected, perform the required immediate pre-takeoff contamination inspection. `[CAR 602.11]`
- `M:` clean-wing concept: no “little bit is fine.”
***
#### Core aircraft documents

| Document | Rule |
|---|---|
| Certificate of Registration | carried on board `[CAR 202.26]` |
| Flight authority / C of A / permit | in effect, conditions complied with, normally carried `[CAR 605.03]` |
| AFM/POH if required | current applicable amendments/supplements available to crew `[CAR 605.04]` |
| Journey log | carried unless a listed exception applies `[CAR 605.95]` |
| Radio licence | carried if one has been issued for onboard apparatus `[CAR 605.03.1]` |

- Journey-log exception: may remain behind when no landing and shutdown is planned anywhere other than departure, subject to exact CAR conditions.
- `T:` proof of insurance is required by CAR 606.02, but “must be carried on board” is not stated as a universal CAR 202/605 document rule.
***
#### Journey log / technical status

- Owner keeps required journey/technical records and empty-W&B report; entries per CAR 605.94/STD 625. `[CAR 605.92]`
- PIC reviews defects, maintenance status/releases, inspections and W&B. `T:` log present≠airworthy.
***
#### Unserviceable equipment — no MEL

- If equipment is required by applicable day/night VFR/IFR standard, manufacturer equipment list, certificate, AD or CAR: no takeoff with it unserviceable/removed. `[CAR 605.10]`
- Non-required defective equipment may permit flight only if isolated/secured, placarded, and recorded as applicable. `[CAR 605.10]`
- PIC cannot fly if safety is affected regardless of deferral mechanics. `[CAR 605.08]`
- `M:` `REQUIRED? → SERVICEABLE? → DEFER LEGALLY? → SAFE?`
***
#### Day VFR equipment — power-driven

- Altimeter (`sensitive/adjustable` in controlled), ASI, independent magnetic direction, required engine/prop gauges, each main-tank fuel quantity, retractable-gear indication and required radio. `[CAR 605.14]`
- `T:` GPS/tablet does not replace required instruments.
***
#### Night VFR equipment

- Day-VFR instruments/equipment specified by 605.16 + sensitive altimeter + turn/slip or turn coordinator + adequate electrical source.
- Also required lighting/instrument illumination/flashlight and other items specified by the full section.
- Position/anti-collision lights used as prescribed. `[CAR 605.16–605.17]`
- `T:` verify the complete current section, not a mnemonic, before dispatching with a defect.
***
#### IFR equipment

- IFR requires the applicable 605.16 equipment plus attitude indicator, VSI, OAT gauge and the remaining equipment/nav/comm items in 605.18.
- Equipment must be adequate for the route/procedure and serviceable.
- `[CAR 605.18]`
- `T:` “IFR-certified GPS installed” ≠ aircraft/procedure/current-database/RAIM/approval requirements all met.
***
#### ELT

- General rule: aircraft must carry required ELT(s) transmitting simultaneously on `406 MHz + 121.5 MHz`, subject to listed exceptions. `[CAR 605.38]`
- Verify registration, battery/inspection status, antenna and remote switch.
- `T:` ELT requirement has detailed aircraft/operation exceptions; do not memorize “always” without checking 605.38.
***
#### Over water · survival / Survol de l’eau · survie

| Memory | Rule / example |
|---|---|
| Flotation trigger | Water takeoff/landing OR unable to reach shore after engine failure: one life preserver, individual flotation device or PFD per person. [602.62(1)] |
| 50 NM / Shore | Landplane >50 NM from shore: LIFE PRESERVER for each person, even if engine-out shore reach is possible. Accessible while seated. [602.62(2),(4)] |
| Raft / Radeau — SE | Single engine, or multi unable to maintain flight with any engine failed: rafts beyond lesser of 100 NM or 30 min at FILED cruise speed from suitable emergency landing site. [602.63(1)] |
| Raft / Radeau — ME | Multi able to maintain flight with any engine failed: lesser of 200 NM/60 min. Transport-category aeroplane exception: up to lesser of 400 NM/120 min. [602.63(2)–(3)] |
| Calculation / Calcul | SE threshold = min(100, filed kt ×0.5) NM. Filed 120 kt →60 NM; 180 kt →90 NM; 240 kt →100 NM. Beyond threshold triggers raft carriage. |
| Capacity / Capacité | Total raft rated capacity covers everyone. Stow accessible, visibly marked near exit; attached kit: shelter, water/purification, visual distress signals, suited to area/season. [602.63(6)] |
| Shore ≠ landing site | Flotation test uses shore reach/distance; raft test uses a SUITABLE EMERGENCY LANDING SITE. Crossing width is not automatically distance from that site. |
| 703 seaplane | 703.83: inflatable flotation worn on/above water; COM procedures and PIC instruction. Exception: person on stretcher, incubator or similar device. Not a blanket rule for every private landplane. |
| Land survival / Survie terre | 602.61: FIRE + SHELTER + WATER + SIGNAL, for everyone and climate. 25-NM exception requires staying within 25 NM of departure aerodrome AND surface-station radio capability throughout. Other listed exceptions exist. |
| Planning / Préparation | Recommendation: wear suitable inflatable flotation, brief evacuation and inflate only outside; assess cold-water exposure and rescue delay. Equipment carriage alone does not make a crossing safe. |
***
#### Oxygen / Oxygène — CAR 605.31–32

| Memory | Rule / example |
|---|---|
| 10–13 / 30 | Cabin-pressure altitude >10,000 to ≤13,000 ft: crew USE oxygen after 30 min. |
| Supply / Réserve | Unpressurized: supply for ALL crew +10% passengers (at least 1 passenger), for time beyond 30 min in that band. |
| Above / Au-dessus 13,000 | All aboard USE oxygen throughout. Unpressurized supply: all aboard, entire time; air transport service minimum 1 hour. |
| Boundaries / Seuils | Exactly 13,000 ft remains in the 30-min band. At 13,100 ft, no 30-min grace. Cabin pressure altitude, not height AGL. |
| Pressurized / Pressurisé | Size emergency supply for worst-point decompression, descent profile and suitable landing aerodrome: use 605.31(2) table and notes; do not reuse unpressurized quantities. |
| Pilot mask / Masque | Pilot at controls: mask required at/above FL250 without quick-donning masks; above FL410 with quick-donning masks. |
***
#### Pre-flight information

- PIC must be familiar with available information appropriate to intended flight. `[CAR 602.71]`
- For flight away from aerodrome vicinity: includes weather, fuel, alternatives, runway/airport suitability, delays and relevant operational information.
- `M:` `WX-NOTAM-FUEL-W&B-PERF-ROUTE-AIRSPACE-RUNWAY-ALTERNATES`.
***
#### Flight plan / itinerary

- IFR: file IFR flight plan, except itinerary permitted in specified cases wholly/partly outside controlled airspace or where facilities inadequate. `[CAR 602.73]`
- VFR: flight plan or itinerary unless flight remains within `25 NM` of departure aerodrome. `[CAR 602.73]`
- International flight: flight plan.
- Flight plan filed with ATS/CARS; itinerary may be filed with responsible person, ATS or CARS. `[CAR 602.75]`
- Report required changes and file arrival report/close as required. `[CAR 602.76–602.78]`
- `T:` landing does not automatically close every plan/itinerary.
***
#### Fuel requirements

| Flight | Required fuel after reaching destination |
|---|---|
| VFR aeroplane day | normal cruise `30 min` |
| VFR aeroplane night | normal cruise `45 min` |
| IFR prop, no alternate | approach + missed + `45 min` |
| IFR prop, alternate | approach + missed → alternate → land + `45 min` |
| IFR turbojet/helicopter | same structure + `30 min` |

- Add taxi and foreseeable delays from weather, routing, traffic, system failure and other foreseeable conditions. `[CAR 602.88]`
- `T:` reserve begins after all required legs; legal reserve is not a target landing fuel.
***
#### Baggage / cargo

- Carry-on baggage, equipment and cargo must be stowed/secured so they cannot shift, obstruct controls/exits or injure occupants. `[CAR 602.86]`
- Respect W&B, baggage-area structural limits and dangerous-goods rules.
- `T:` within total weight ≠ correctly secured or within CG.
***
#### Passenger briefing

- Before takeoff brief exits, seat belts/harnesses/restraints, seats/tables, baggage, oxygen where applicable and smoking restrictions. `[CAR 602.89]`
- Where applicable include life preservers, first-aid/survival equipment, ELT and raft information.
- `M:` brief normal use + emergency action, not just “seat belt on.”
***
#### Safety belts / restraints

- Seats, safety belts and restraint systems must meet 605.22–605.28 requirements.
- PIC ensures passengers are instructed and comply during required phases/conditions.
- `T:` one belt for two persons is not the default; child-restraint provisions are specific.
***
#### Right-of-way / collision avoidance

- Distress has priority; ROW never removes collision-avoidance duty. `[CAR 602.19; 602.21]`
- Converging: traffic on right has ROW; head-on: both right; overtaking: pass/keep clear right.
- Powered aeroplane gives way to airship/glider/balloon/towing aircraft; landing aircraft has ROW, but lower aircraft may not cut in on final.
- `M:` `RIGHT-RIGHT-RIGHT`. `T:` ATC clearance does not remove see-and-avoid in VMC.
***
#### Minimum altitudes / distances

- Built-up area/open-air assembly: altitude allowing emergency landing without hazard and aeroplane at least `1000 ft` above highest obstacle within `2000 ft` horizontally. `[CAR 602.14]`
- Elsewhere: at least `500 ft` from any person, vessel, vehicle or structure. `[CAR 602.14]`
- Exceptions include takeoff/approach/landing and authorized low-altitude operations under 602.15.
- `T:` “500 AGL” is not the wording; it is `500 ft from` persons/vessels/vehicles/structures.
***
#### Aerobatic manoeuvres

- Restrictions include designated airspace/areas, minimum visibility, cloud clearance, altitude and passenger conditions. `[CAR 602.27–602.28]`
- Aircraft POH/category/W&B limitations also apply.
- `T:` POH-approved spin ≠ legal anywhere/any altitude/with passengers.
***
#### ATC clearances / instructions

- Comply with ATC clearances/instructions received and accepted, except emergency authority or amended clearance. `[CAR 602.31]`
- If unable/unsafe: advise ATC immediately; do not silently improvise.
- `T:` clearance ≠ information; “roger” does not mean approval to deviate.
***
#### Cruising altitudes

- At more than `3000 ft AGL`, use appropriate cruising altitude/flight level based on track, unless otherwise authorized/required. `[CAR 602.34]`
- VFR: `000–179°` odd thousands +`500`; `180–359°` even thousands +`500`.
- Southern Domestic Airspace uses magnetic track; Northern Domestic Airspace true track.
- `T:` track, not heading.
***
#### Aerodrome / MF operations

- Ensure suitability/no collision; observe/conform to or avoid circuit; left turns unless CFS/ATC says right; comply CFS/NOTAM; into wind where practicable. Controlled aerodrome: clearance to taxi/TKOF/land. `[CAR 602.96]`
- No flight below `2000 ft over an aerodrome` except listed operations/ATC authorization; separate from circuit and CAR 602.14 minima. `[CAR 602.96(4)]`
- MF procedures/reports: CAR 602.97–602.104 + current CFS frequency/area/hours. `T:` uncontrolled≠unregulated; ATF calls≠MF mandatory reports.
***
#### VFR minima — controlled

- Visual reference to surface.
- Flight visibility `≥3 SM`.
- Cloud distance `≥500 ft vertical + 1 SM horizontal`.
- In control zone: reported ground visibility `≥3 SM`; except takeoff/landing, aircraft `≥500 ft` from surface.
- `[CAR 602.114]`
***
#### VFR minima — uncontrolled aeroplane

| Height | Day | Night | Cloud |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `≥1000 AGL` | `1 SM` | `3 SM` | `500 ft vert / 2000 ft horiz` |
| `<1000 AGL` | `2 SM` | `3 SM` | clear of cloud |

- Visual reference to surface required. `[CAR 602.115]`
- `T:` these are legal minima, not personal minima.
***
#### Special VFR

- Only within a control zone; pilot requests and obtains ATC authorization.
- Aeroplane flight visibility `≥1 SM`; helicopter `≥1/2 SM`.
- Remain clear of cloud and in visual reference to surface. Reported ground visibility and radio conditions also apply. Night aeroplane SVFR authorization is to land at destination; do not generalize it to night departures. `[CAR 602.117; AIM RAC 2.7.3]`
- `T:` SVFR is not permission to enter cloud/IMC; ATC traffic capacity and conditions still apply.
***
#### VFR OTT

- Day cruise only; vertical cloud separation `≥1000 ft`; between layers at least `5000 ft`; flight visibility `≥5 SM`; destination weather and rating/equipment conditions apply. `[CAR 602.116; 401.44–401.45; 605.15]`
- `T:` PPL alone does not automatically include VFR OTT privilege.
***
#### IFR general

- IFR operation requires IFR flight plan/clearance where applicable, instrument rating/current privileges, appropriate equipment and compliance with IFR minima/procedures. `[CAR 602.121+]`
- Alternate, takeoff, approach, landing and approach-ban rules are separated in CAR 602.122–602.131.
- `T:` advisory visibility, approach-ban threshold and DA/MDA visual-reference rule are three different concepts.
***
#### Airspace entry memory

| Class | VFR memory |
|---|---|
| A | IFR only |
| B | ATC clearance + specified equipment/qualification |
| C | ATC clearance before entry |
| D | establish two-way communication before entry |
| E | no VFR clearance generally; IFR controlled |
| F | special-use rules: `CYR/CYA/CYD` |
| G | uncontrolled; CARs still apply |

- Check current chart/CFS for floor, ceiling, transponder and communication requirements.
***
#### Transponder emergency codes

- `1200` VFR at/below `12,500 ASL`; `1400` VFR above `12,500`, unless assigned otherwise.
- `7500` unlawful interference | `7600` communication failure | `7700` emergency.
- `T:` current CFS/AIM/ATC assignment wins; Mode C/pressure altitude normally remains ON unless directed.
***
#### Night definition

- `Night` = period from end of evening civil twilight to beginning of morning civil twilight. `[CAR 101.01]`
- `T:` sunset is not the legal start of night; use official twilight data.
***
#### Reporting occurrences / accidents

- TSB reporting obligations are under Transportation Safety Board Regulations; preserve evidence/notify authorities as applicable.
- CAR emergency actions, ELT use and overdue-aircraft reporting may also apply.
- `T:` “incident” in common speech ≠ reportable aviation occurrence definition.
***
#### High-yield traps

- PIC vs ATC | PPL cost-share conditions | CPL≠AOC | medical vs `5y/24mo/6mo` recency | night passenger recency | `500 ft from`≠`500 AGL`.
- Controlled `3-1-500`; uncontrolled high `1D/3N-2000/500`; low `2D/3N-clear`; `25 NM` plan exemption; fuel `30D/45N`; MF≠ATF; SVFR pilot-request/control-zone/COC/surface.
***
#### Pebble

- `PIC responsible / emergency only as necessary`
- fit + `12h bottle-to-throttle`
- recency `5y / 24mo / pax 5TO+5LDG in 6mo / IFR 24mo check; after six months, 6 apps in 6mo; no 6h minimum`
