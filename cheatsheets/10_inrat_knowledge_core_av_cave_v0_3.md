#### INRAT attack method

- Read question stem first: asks `legal?`, `safe?`, `next?`, `minimum?`, `forecast?`, `plate?`, `lost comm?`.
- Extract: aircraft category, equipment, departure/dest/alternate, ETA, route, freezing level, winds, NOTAM, CAP note, RASS, lighting, RVR/vis, TAF change group.
- `M:` source order = CAR/STD → CAP/CFS/NOTAM → AIM/TP guide → school notes.
- `T:` many wrong answers are “operationally plausible but legally wrong” or “legal but unsafe.”
***
#### Air law / IFR privileges

- IFR needs appropriate licence/rating/medical/recency + aircraft equipment + IFR flight plan/clearance where required.
- IFR rating privilege depends on group/class/category and aircraft. `G3` single only; `G1` covers all aeroplanes.
- `T:` INRAT pass≠rating issued; written + experience + skill + admin still needed.
- `T:` clearance limit is not destination authorization beyond that point; expect hold/further clearance.
***
#### IFR fuel — CAR 602.88 memory

- IFR prop with alternate: dest + approach + missed → alternate + land → `45 min`.
- IFR prop no alternate: dest + approach + missed → `45 min`.
- IFR turbojet/helicopter: same logic but `30 min`.
- Always add foreseeable taxi/delay/met/routing/traffic/other delays.
- `T:` fuel reserve is after required legs, not “fuel on board at destination.” Exam may hide missed approach before alternate leg.
***
#### IFR flight planning workflow

- Route: CFS/CAP/LO chart/preferred routes/airways/direct fixes/MEA/MOCA/AMA/MSA/mountainous region/controlled airspace.
- Weather: METAR/SPECI actual; TAF legality; GFA area; FD winds/temp; SIGMET/AIRMET/PIREP; NOTAM; RSC/CRFI; freezing level.
- Leg calc: `TC±VAR=MC±DEV=CH`; `time=dist/GS`; `fuel=time×burn`; `ETA=dep+ETE`; `wind correction` and actual GS.
- `T:` simulated flight questions often test route feasibility, not one isolated fact.
***
#### IFR route / altitude terms

| Term | Memory |
|---|---|
| `MEA` | obstacle + nav reception on airway/route |
| `MOCA` | obstacle clearance; nav reception usually limited near NAVAID |
| `MRA` | min altitude to receive/use fix-defining signal |
| `AMA` | area min altitude; replaced old `GASA`; standard temp/pressure |
| `MSA` | emergency/sector altitude near facility/ARP/waypoint |
| `MVA` | ATC radar/vector altitude, normally unpublished to pilots |

- `T:` cleared direct ≠ automatically safe at low altitude; compare minimum IFR altitude / terrain / cold temp / MEA logic.
***
#### Altimeter-setting / standard-pressure regions

- Altimeter-setting region: departure set aerodrome setting/elevation; enroute nearest station along route; arrival set destination setting if available.
- Standard-pressure region: set `29.92/1013.2` except departure/arrival transition procedures.
- Climbing into standard-pressure region: set standard immediately after entering. Descending into altimeter-setting region: set appropriate station immediately before descending into region.
- `T:` flight level = standard-pressure altimeter. “high-to-low/cold = look out below” still applies.
***
#### Controlled / uncontrolled IFR

- Controlled IFR: clearance, altitude, route, comms, transponder/Mode C where required.
- Uncontrolled IFR: pilot self-separation from terrain and other IFR/VFR using published procedures, MF/ATF calls, IFR flight plan/itinerary, and weather/altitude minima.
- `T:` uncontrolled IFR departure/arrival may have no ATC separation until controlled airspace; broadcast and protect yourself.
***
#### Clearance / readback

- Clearance contents: clearance limit, route, altitude, departure/restrictions, frequency, squawk, void/release/hold-for-release if issued.
- `P:` read back clearance limit/route/altitude/restrictions/squawk/hold-short/runway.
- `T:` “cleared for the approach” authorizes IAP but not visual descent below minima; “cleared to airport” may be clearance limit, not landing clearance.
***
#### IFR in VMC / visual restriction

- If IFR clearance includes VFR/visual restriction, pilot accepts own terrain/traffic/wake responsibility as specified.
- Visual approach: IFR remains IFR; visual reference to airport/traffic; ATC may clear visual; missed usually as coordinated/ATC, not necessarily published IAP missed unless assigned.
- Contact approach: pilot requests; clear of cloud; flight visibility and reasonable expectation to destination; IFR remains IFR.
- `T:` visual/contact approach can remove published segment protection if you wander.
***
#### Lost comms — general

- `P:` squawk `7600`; maintain listening watch; try alternate freq/FSS/relay/phone when practical; comply with specific SID/STAR/IAP/CAP comm-fail instructions.
- `T:` specific published comm-fail procedure overrides generic rules.
- `T:` if ATC says “if no comm by X, expect Y,” that can become expected route/alt/time logic.
***
#### Lost comms — VMC / IMC

- VMC after failure: continue VFR and land as soon as practicable. `T:` practicable≠nearest unsuitable field.
- IMC route: `A-V-E-F` = assigned → vectored direct to fix/route/airway → expected → filed.
- IMC altitude: highest of `A-M-E` = assigned / minimum IFR altitude / expected. If vectored below published IFR altitude, climb immediately to appropriate minimum IFR altitude.
- Descent: maintain enroute altitude to nav facility/approach fix; descend at latest ETA/EAT/EFC logic. If holding instruction acknowledged, hold as directed and approach at `EAT/EFC`.
- `M:` lost comm = `7600 + VMC land / IMC AVEF + AME + latest time`.
***
#### Departure / takeoff minima

- Check CAP aerodrome chart/SID/ODP/diverse departure, climb gradient, obstacle notes, RVR/visibility, lighting, engine-out capability if applicable.
- `R:` takeoff visibility precedence: use runway `RVR` when reported at/above the applicable minimum; use aerodrome `ground visibility` when RVR is below minimum, varies below/above minimum, or is not reported; use PIC-observed `runway visibility` only when neither RVR nor ground visibility is reported. `[CAR 602.126]`
- Applicable minimum comes from the AOC/special authorization, or the current CAP for other operations.
- `T:` takeoff visibility ≠ landing/approach visibility. Departure obstacle climb gradient may be more restrictive than weather.
- `T:` “standard takeoff minima” is not “safe to climb over obstacles”; compare required climb gradient vs aircraft performance.
***
#### SID / ODP / climb gradient

- SID = published ATC/obstacle/departure routing; may include altitude/speed/climb gradient.
- Diverse departure assumes obstacle environment supports generic departure; if obstacle DP exists, use it unless ATC assigns otherwise.
- Climb gradient: `ft/NM`; required ROC ≈ `GS × ft/NM / 60`.
- Common 3.3% ≈ `200 ft/NM`. `F:` `ROC fpm = GS kt × gradient(ft/NM)/60`.
- `T:` climb gradient is ground-speed based; tailwind/high GS raises required fpm.
***
#### Enroute / position reports / ATC

- IFR position report when required: position, time, altitude, next fix/ETA, ensuing fix, remarks.
- STAR lateral path forms part of route when filed/cleared; descent clearance still needed unless specifically cleared by ATC/procedure.
- ATC speed assignment supersedes charted speed until prohibited/cancelled; charted restrictions remain unless cancelled.
- `T:` STAR vertical restrictions above assigned altitude remain mandatory unless cancelled.
***
#### Holding

- Clearance: fix, direction, radial/course/bearing/airway, leg length/time, altitude, EFC.
- Standard = right turns; non-standard = left. Published max speed inside hold wins.
- Entries: direct / teardrop / parallel. `T:` entry is a method; protected airspace matters.
- Timing: standard outbound leg `1 min` at/below `14,000 ft ASL`; `1.5 min` above. Adjust outbound to achieve the corresponding inbound time; DME holds use specified distances. `[AIM 2026-1 RAC 10.3/10.6]`
- Wind: outbound correction about `3×` inbound drift. `T:` EFC and fuel are exam-critical.
***
#### Publication-currency exam traps

- Check effective date/cycle first. Then NOTAM and AIP SUP; Trigger NOTAM only points to changed material.
- AIP SUP can remain active with no current Trigger NOTAM.
- PFAF lightning-bolt depiction is a future incremental standard beginning `2026-10-29`; current July-cycle questions use current-chart symbology.
- `T:` wrong-cycle plate may produce a numerically correct but operationally invalid answer.

#### CAP plate read flow

- `P:` title/effective→airport/runway/proc→nav source/freq/course→MSA/Safe100/terrain→RASS/cold temp→IAF/IF/FAF/FAP→stepdowns→DA/MDA/HAT/HAA/HATh/DH→advisory vis→MAP→missed→lighting/runway/LDA→notes.
- `T:` wrong frequency/source/course = common trap. Always verify CDI source and loaded procedure.
- `T:` plan view altitude is not enough; profile and minima notes can modify it.
***
#### CAP definitions / minima

| Item | Memory |
|---|---|
| `DA` | altitude where missed shall be initiated if visual ref not established |
| `DH` | height of DA above TDZE/threshold |
| `MDA` | no descent below until required visual ref |
| `HAA` | MDA above aerodrome elevation, circling |
| `HAT/HATh` | DA/MDA above TDZE/threshold, straight-in |
| `MSA` | min 1000 ft clearance in sector, standard temp/pressure |
| `RVR` | runway centreline visual range of markings/lights |

- `T:` advisory visibility helps probability/ban logic; DA/MDA controls vertical descent.
***
#### RASS / remote altimeter

- `Use CYND`: procedure minima have RASS built in; use identified altimeter source.
- `When using CYND add 160`: add adjustment to intermediate, final, and missed-approach segment minimum altitudes.
- `When using CYND add 160. Circling minima apply`: final descent gradient exceeded after RASS; straight-in minima not usable.
- `T:` RASS/cold temperature corrections can change stepdowns, MDA/DA, and missed segment altitudes; do not just add to the final line mentally.
***
#### Approach types

- Precision: ILS with lateral+vertical guidance; uses `DA/DH`.
- APV: vertical guidance but not precision; e.g., `LPV`, `LNAV/VNAV`; uses `DA`.
- NPA: lateral guidance only; e.g., LOC/VOR/NDB/LNAV; uses `MDA`.
- SCDA/CDFA: stable descent technique on NPA; do not descend below MDA without visual reference.
- `T:` LPV is not ILS. Baro-VNAV temperature limits matter; uncompensated altimetry can invalidate VNAV minima.
***
#### ILS / LOC / VOR / NDB / DME

- ILS: localizer lateral + glideslope vertical; identify frequency/course; beware false GS from above.
- LOC: localizer lateral only; no glideslope; use LOC minima/MDA.
- VOR: radials FROM station; set correct course; TO/FROM flag matters.
- NDB/ADF: bearing to station; wind correction/relative bearing; high workload; needle points to station, not necessarily desired track.
- DME: slant range; DME arcs require lead/radial orientation.
- `T:` reverse sensing only if display/source/course logic mishandled; know equipment.
***
#### RNAV/GNSS

- Verify database current, correct airport/runway/procedure/transition, RAIM/WAAS, mode/sensitivity, OBS/SUSP, missed approach sequencing.
- CDI sensitivity: enroute → terminal → approach; ensure unit arms/activates correct mode.
- Alternate planning with GNSS: verify availability/integrity and current Canadian rules; ground NAVAID fallback may be required depending equipment/approval.
- `T:` “direct-to FAF” may delete procedure sequencing/protection. `OBS/SUSP` misuse is an INRAT classic.
***
#### Descent / approach math

- `F:` 3° path ≈ `300 ft/NM`; required ROD ≈ `GS × 5`.
- `F:` descent gradient ft/NM → `ROC/ROD fpm = GS × ft/NM / 60`.
- `F:` descent distance NM ≈ altitude to lose / `300` for 3°. `T:` add level segments, stepdowns, wind, configuration.
- `F:` time min = distance/GS×60. `T:` groundspeed, not IAS.
***
#### Landing minima / visual reference

- Do not continue below DA/DH or descend below MDA unless required visual reference is established and maintained for safe landing.
- Required visual refs include runway/markings/threshold/TDZ/approach lights/VGSI/RIL/threshold-end lights/edge/centreline/TDZL.
- `T:` seeing “some lights” may not be enough if it doesn’t allow position/rate assessment for safe landing.
***
#### Missed approach

- Precision/APV: at DA/DH without visual refs → missed.
- NPA: at MAP or when unable to continue safely/legally → missed; do not descend below MDA before visual refs.
- Fly published missed exactly: climb, turn limits, nav source, altitude, hold. If missed after MAP, obstacle clearance may not be assured.
- `T:` power/attitude/config first; then nav. brief missed before FAF.
***
#### Circling

- Circling minima based on category; maintain visual reference; remain within protected area; use published restrictions.
- If visual reference lost while circling: initiate missed; generally turn toward landing runway and intercept published missed unless procedure/SOP says otherwise.
- `T:` circling at night/marginal wx is high risk; exam may test category/speed/minima mismatch.
***
#### Approach ban / RVR — general aviation

- GA NPA/APV/CAT I/II: governed by RVR values when runway served by RVR.
- General non-Part VII aeroplane rule: if both measured, below minimum when `RVR A <1200` **or** `RVR B <600`; if only one is measured, below minimum when it is `<1200`. `[CAR 602.129]`
- Exceptions include: below-min RVR received after FAF; training missed approach; RVR varying below/above; RVR below min but ground vis ≥`1/4 SM`; CAT III as applicable.
- `T:` if RVR unavailable/not reported, GA approach-ban criterion may not exist; still need DA/MDA visual reference to land.
- `T:` commercial approach-ban rules are stricter/different; don’t mix GA and 703/704/705 tables.
***
#### Takeoff visibility / RVR trap

- RVR report can be below takeoff minimum but ground visibility at/above minimum may govern; inverse also possible.
- If both lowest RVR and ground visibility are below required minimum, takeoff not authorized.
- If only RVR below minimum and no governing ground visibility at/above minimum, takeoff not authorized.
- `T:` “variable RVR” and “observed on-the-hour vs now” wording can flip the answer.
***
#### Alternate minima core

| Facility at alternate | Weather memory |
|---|---|
| 2+ usable precision, separate suitable runways | `400-1` or `+200/+1/2` above lowest usable HAT/vis, greater |
| 1 usable precision | `600-2` or `+300/+1` above lowest HAT/vis, greater |
| NPA only | `800-2` or `+300/+1` above lowest HAT/HAA and vis, greater |
| No IFR approach | forecast ≥`500 ft` above min IFR altitude permitting VFR app/landing |

- `600-2` and `800-2` are standard alternate minima. If standard applies, other authorized minima may be available from CAP table.
- `T:` “alternate not authorized” / NOTAM NAVAID out / runway not suitable / no forecast can kill an otherwise good alternate.
***
#### TAF alternate logic

- Improving `BECMG`: use new condition at END of BECMG period.
- Deteriorating `BECMG`: use new condition at START of BECMG period.
- `TEMPO` must not be below alternate minima.
- `PROB` must not be below appropriate landing minima.
- GFA alternate: no cloud <`1000 ft` above lowest usable HAT/HAA; no CB; vis ≥`3 SM`.
- Aerodrome advisory forecast alternate: ≥`500 ft` above lowest usable HAT/HAA and vis ≥`3 SM`.
- `T:` alternate minima ≠ landing minima ≠ approach-ban minima.
***
#### Weather decoding — exam workflow

- METAR/SPECI: wind, vis, wx, cloud, temp/dew, altimeter, remarks; RVR if vis ≤`1 SM` and/or RVR ≤`6000 ft`.
- TAF: validity, FM, BECMG, TEMPO, PROB, wind/vis/wx/cloud. Ceiling = lowest BKN/OVC/VV.
- FD: interpolate wind/temp aloft; choose true altitude/level; use winds for GS/fuel/temp/icing.
- GFA: clouds/wx + icing/turb/freezing level; use time panels and expected movement.
- `T:` TAF legal but GFA/SIGMET/PIREPs ugly = operational no-go even if exam legal answer exists.
***
#### IFR weather traps

- Freezing level + cloud/precip = icing risk. Carb ice can exist above freezing in humid partial power.
- Warm front: layered cloud/low vis/steady precip/freezing rain risk. Cold front: convective showers/TS/gusts/wind shear.
- TS/CB: avoid; embedded CB in IMC is no-go for light trainer.
- Low-level wind shear/microburst: go-around may not save you if severe; avoid.
- `T:` altimetry errors from cold temps affect obstacle clearance, not just indicated altitude comfort.
***
#### Instrument failures

- Pitot blocked + drain open: ASI reads `0`. Pitot blocked + drain blocked: ASI acts altimeter-ish. Static blocked: ASI/ALT/VSI errors; alternate static changes readings.
- Vacuum fail: attitude/heading unreliable; use turn coordinator/compass/power/performance if electric TC.
- Compass: variation, deviation, dip, acceleration, turning errors. `ANDS/UNOS` for N hemisphere.
- Electrical fail: load shed, battery time, comm/nav loss planning. `T:` GPS/iPad not a full IFR electrical system.
***
#### Navigation aid checks

- VOR: identify Morse/voice, correct freq/course/TO-FROM, service volume and NOTAM. Dual-receiver comparison difference `>4°` means one may be beyond acceptable tolerance; investigate before IFR. Airborne landmark/published-radial check error `>±6°` ⇒ do not use for IFR navigation. `[AIM 2026-1 COM 4.5.1–4.5.2]`
- ILS: identify LOC; set front course; GS alive from below; marker/DME/fixes per plate.
- NDB: identify; station passage/needle swing; ADF errors near TS/coast/night/mountain.
- GNSS: current DB, RAIM/WAAS, correct mode, CDI scaling, annunciations.
- `T:` failed identification = unusable navaid for approach.
***
#### Human factors / single-pilot IFR

- Threats: workload, fixation, automation surprise, spatial disorientation, fatigue, hypoxia, CO, pressure.
- `P:` brief before busy segment; set bugs/freqs/GPS/OBS; use flows+checklists; say unable early.
- Leans/coriolis/graveyard spiral/somatogravic illusion: trust instruments, cross-check, standard-rate turns.
- `T:` automation can be wrong because pilot loaded it wrong.
***
#### INRAT high-yield traps

- Legal source vs guidance source. RVR vs ground vis vs advisory vis. Alternate minima vs landing minima vs approach ban. RASS/cold-temp corrections.
- Lost comm route/alt/time. STAR lateral vs vertical clearance. Visual/contact approach IFR status. Hold EFC/fuel.
- MAP vs MDA vs missed after MAP. SCDA not permission below MDA. LPV vs ILS. LNAV/VNAV temp limits. OBS/SUSP.
- Fuel includes approach+missed at destination before alternate. `TEMPO/BECMG/PROB` logic. Uncontrolled IFR comms/separation. NOTAM invalidates NAVAID/runway/procedure.
