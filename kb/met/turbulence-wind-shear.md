# Turbulence, wind shear & mountain wave / Turbulence, cisaillement et ondes orographiques
<!-- concept: met-turb | level: PPL CPL IR -->

[Meteorology index / Index météo](README.md) · concept `met-turb` · sources, legend and release status in the index. Unit IDs are stable: never rename one.

***
#### Turbulence
<!-- id: wx-turbulence | from: 16 -->

- Mechanical: wind over terrain/buildings; strongest low/lee side.
- Convective: thermal/CU/TS.
- Frontal: wind/shear + convection.
- Mountain wave/rotor: strong stable cross-ridge flow.
- CAT: often near jet stream/strong wind shear, without cloud.
- `T:` turbulence intensity depends on aircraft response; same air mass affects light aircraft more.
***
#### Mountain wave
<!-- id: wx-mountain-wave | from: 16 -->

- Favoured by strong wind roughly perpendicular to ridge + stable layer.
- Smooth wave aloft; severe rotor/turbulence below/lee; strong up/downdrafts.
- Lenticular `ACSL` = wave clue; rotor cloud may mark turbulence.
- `!:` downdraft may exceed climb capability.
***
#### Wind shear
<!-- id: wx-wind-shear | from: 16 -->

- Rapid wind speed/direction change over short distance.
- Sources: fronts, inversions/low-level jets, terrain, TS/microbursts.
- Low-level shear changes IAS/flight path quickly near ground.
- `T:` surface wind can look acceptable while shear above is severe.
***
#### Microburst / LLWS
<!-- id: wx-microburst | from: 16 -->

- Microburst = concentrated downdraft spreading outward at surface.
- Aircraft may see: headwind/IAS↑ → downdraft → tailwind/IAS↓.
- Most dangerous low during takeoff/landing.
- Virga, heavy showers, gust fronts and TS are clues.
- `M:` gain first can precede severe loss.
***
#### Jet stream / CAT
<!-- id: wx-jet-cat | from: 16 | also: met-systems -->

- Narrow band of strong upper wind near tropopause, usually strongest horizontal temp gradient.
- CAT favoured near jet core/shear zones, especially bends/entrance/exit regions.
- Jet position helps locate fronts/system movement.
***
#### Turbulence · QRC
<!-- id: wxp-turbulence | from: 05 -->

- Mechanical: strong wind over rough terrain/obstacles; worst low/lee.
- Convective: CU/TCU/CB/showers. Mountain wave: strong cross-ridge stable flow. CAT: jet-stream/wind-shear zones aloft.
- `P:` choose altitude/route/time; slow to turbulence penetration speed per POH; secure cabin; report.
- `T:` Va changes with weight; published max-gross Va may be too high at lower weight.
***
#### LLWS / microburst
<!-- id: wxp-llws | from: 05 -->

- TAF wind shear group describes significant shear in lowest `1500 ft AGL`.
- Red flags: TS/gust front, virga, strong inversion/low-level jet, large surface-vs-aloft wind difference.
- `!:` postpone/avoid severe shear; go-around may enter worse performance.
- `T:` increasing airspeed followed by rapid loss can be microburst signature; follow aircraft procedure.
***
#### Mountain / terrain weather
<!-- id: wxp-mountain | from: 05 -->

- Check ridge wind angle/speed, mountain wave, rotor, obscuration, ceiling above pass, escape route, downdraft performance.
- `P:` cross ridge with margin/angle/escape; avoid lee rotor/cloud; no narrow-valley commitment without turnaround room.
- `T:` reported aerodrome ceiling may not clear enroute terrain.
