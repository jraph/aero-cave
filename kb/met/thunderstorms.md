#### Thunderstorms — ingredients
<!-- id: wx-thunderstorms | from: 16 -->

- Moisture + instability + lift.
- Stages:
  1. **Cumulus:** updrafts only.
  2. **Mature:** up + downdrafts; precip; maximum hazards.
  3. **Dissipating:** downdrafts dominate.
- Hazards: extreme turbulence, hail, lightning, heavy precip, icing, tornado, wind shear/microburst.
- `!:` avoid; never use a visual/radar “gap” to thread active cells.
***
#### Convection gate
<!-- id: wxp-convection | from: 05 | also: met-decide -->

- Hazards: extreme turb/hail/lightning/ice/heavy precip/shear-microburst/tornado/instrument errors.
- `!:` large-margin avoidance of cells/lines/embedded TS; never thread a radar gap.
- Assess SIGMET/GFA/TAF/PIREP/radar/lightning/satellite for movement/growth/line/escape. `T:` datalink delayed; attenuation hides cells.
