#### Performance / W&B
<!-- id: agk-performance-w-b | from: 01 | also: agk-perf | level: PPL CPL -->

- Vx=altitude/distance. Vy=altitude/time. Best glide=max distance no power. Min sink=max time.
- Headwind helps; tailwind hurts big. grass/soft/wet/snow roll↑ braking↓. +10% speed≈+21% energy.
- `F:` moment=`W×arm`; CG=`ΣM/ΣW`. `T:` within max weight≠within CG.
- FWD CG: stable↑ stall↑ elevator/flare↓. AFT CG: stable↓ stall/spin recovery worse.

#### Performance / W&B · card 03
<!-- id: agk-performance-w-b-c03 | from: 03 | also: agk-perf | level: CPL -->

- Calculate, don’t guess. TO/LDG: weight/temp/pressure/elev/wind/slope/surface/config/technique.
- `M:` hot-high-heavy-humid-tailwind-soft-uphill TO = bad stack.
- LDG energy: KE∝V²; +10% speed≈+21% energy. Tailwind small value, large distance penalty.
- CG=`ΣM/ΣW`; check takeoff+landing+zero-fuel if near edge. Aft CG = recovery worse; forward CG = flare authority lower.

#### Weight / balance
<!-- id: agk-weight-balance | from: 06 | also: study-formulas | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- `F:` `moment = weight × arm`.
- `F:` `CG = Σmoment / Σweight`.
- `F:` `new CG = (old moment ± added/removed/shifted moment) / new weight`.
- `F:` shifted load moment change = `weight × distance shifted`.
- `M:` forward load lowers arm? depends datum. Calculate, don’t feel it.
- `T:` within max gross ≠ within CG envelope. Check takeoff + landing + zero-fuel if near limit.
