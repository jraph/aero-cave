#### Wildlife hazards and conservation — AIR 2.4.1 / RAC 1.10
<!-- id: agk-wildlife-hazards-conservation | level: CPL -->

- Birds mostly **<500 ft**; in migration **2 500–3 000 ft**, some to **20 000 ft**. Most strikes in TKOF/LDG; damage rises with speed → avoid low altitude where possible. [AIR 2.4.1]
- `R` **≥2 000 ft AGL** over: fur and poultry farms (fur farms: chrome-yellow/black stripes, red flag whelping Feb–May) · wildlife herds · wildlife refuges and bird sanctuaries on charts · national/provincial/municipal parks and reserves. [RAC 1.10]
- `R` Intentionally killing migratory birds with an aircraft is prohibited (Migratory Birds Regulations). TKOF/LDG in a bird sanctuary may need a permit; in national parks only at prescribed places (National Parks of Canada Aircraft Access Regulations).

- Sources: [TC AIM AIR](https://tc.canada.ca/sites/default/files/2026-10/aim-2026-2_air_en.pdf); [TC AIM RAC 1.10](https://tc.canada.ca/sites/default/files/2026-10/aim-2026-2_rac_en.pdf).

#### Porpoising (seaplane)
<!-- id: agk-porpoising-seaplane | level: CPL -->

- Porpoising = **rhythmic pitch oscillation** on the step during water TKOF or LDG, usually from **wrong pitch attitude** (too low or too high on the step). Can grow until the nose digs in.
- `P` Recovery: set and hold the correct planing attitude; if it continues or grows, **close throttle** and hold attitude (TKOF); on LDG apply power and go around rather than chase it.
