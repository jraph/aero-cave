#### Situational awareness and cockpit setup
<!-- id: agk-situational-awareness-cockpit-setup | also: ifr | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- Situational awareness = awareness of all information relevant to the current situation; first step of decision-making. [TC Human Factors for Aviation, Advanced Handbook TP 12864]
- Perceive → understand → project (what comes next). Low SA precedes most accidents (CFIT, LOC-I, mid-air). Fatigue, stress and environment shrink SA capacity day to day. [TC ASL 1/2019]
- Keep SA: split the flight into segments; before each, name the top threats + countermeasures; re-prioritize at each segment change; checklists cover equipment, not this flight's threats; ask yourself "how is my SA?" [ASL 1/2019]
- Seat: eyes at **Design Eye Reference Point** (manufacturer marks, e.g. glare-shield balls); instruments + warning lights unobscured; full control travel; nose not blocking view on approach. [AIM AIR 4.12]
- Disorientation: rely on flight instruments; never senses alone. [AIR 3.7]

#### Workload, information processing, TEM and undesired aircraft states
<!-- id: agk-workload-information-processing-tem-uas | level: CPL -->

- Pilot performance loop: **gather information → process → decide → act**; actions change the situation and the loop repeats, often several at once. Workload rises when inputs exceed processing capacity → task shedding, fixation, missed items. [TC Human Factors for Aviation, Basic Handbook TP 12863]
- **TEM**: analyse hazards and act to avoid, trap or mitigate **threats** and manage **errors** before they lead to an **undesired aircraft state**. [TC AC 700-042]
- **Undesired aircraft state (UAS)**: crew-induced position or speed deviation, misapplied flight controls, or incorrect systems configuration that reduces safety margins; last chance before an incident/accident.
- `T` Once in a UAS, switch from fixing the error to **managing the aircraft state** first (fly, then diagnose).

- Sources: [TC HF module](https://bac-lac.wayback.archive-it.org/web/20071219042208/http:/www.tc.gc.ca/civilaviation/systemsafety/pubs/pdm/module2.htm); [TEM in flight operations (ICAO model)](https://skybrary.aero/articles/threat-and-error-management-tem-flight-operations).
