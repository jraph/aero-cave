#### RASS / remote altimeter
<!-- id: ifr-rass-remote-altimeter | from: 09, 10 -->

- Before IAP: current usable altimeter required; local or authorized remote. older than 60min caution; 90min current limit concept.
- `Use CYND`: procedure minima have RASS built in; use identified altimeter source.
- `When using CYND add 160`: add adjustment to intermediate, final, and missed-approach segment minimum altitudes.
- `When using CYND add 160. Circling minima apply`: final descent gradient exceeded after RASS; straight-in minima not usable.
- `T:` RASS/cold/remote/falling-pressure corrections affect obstacle margin: they can change stepdowns, MDA/DA, and missed segment altitudes; do not just add to the final line mentally.
- Read the altimeter source line before the minima: the approved source drives procedure protection.
