#### Temperature
<!-- id: calc-temperature | from: 06 | kind: formula | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- `F:` `°C = (°F - 32) × 5/9`.
- `F:` `°F = °C × 9/5 + 32`.
- `M:` `0°C=32°F`; `10°C=50°F`; `20°C=68°F`; `30°C=86°F`.
- `T:` cold temp correction affects obstacle clearance; not just comfort.
