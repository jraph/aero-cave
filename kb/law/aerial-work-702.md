#### Scope and flight operations — 702.01 / .11 / .13 / .14 / .16–.18 / .20 / .23 / .24
<!-- id: law-702-scope-flight-operations | level: CPL -->

- `R` 702 applies to aeroplane or helicopter aerial work with: persons other than flight crew on board · helicopter Class B/C/D external loads · towing objects · dispersing products. **Not**: ultra-lights, sightseeing.
- `R` Ops personnel instructed on duties and follow the COM. Flight starts only when **authorized per COM**; operational flight plan (Commercial Air Service Standards) prepared per COM.
- `R` Non-crew persons carried only if: presence **essential** during flight · parachutist + AOC authorizes parachute descents · or AOC authorizes carrying persons + CASS met.
- `R` Day VFR, uncontrolled, <1 000 ft AGL: aeroplane may fly with flight visibility **<2 SM**, helicopter **<1 SM**, only with **AOC authorization + CASS**.
- `R` Night, VFR OTT or IFR **not** while towing, carrying heli Class B/C/D load, dispersing, or in a **single-engined** ACFT, unless AOC authorizes + CASS.
- `R` Night with non-crew persons only if: PIC holds an **instrument rating** · or parachutists, AOC for parachuting + night VFR, within **10 NM** of DEP A/D · or AOC authorizes carrying persons + CASS.
- `R` Land ACFT over water beyond gliding reach of shore after engine failure (except TKOF/LDG) → AOC authorization + CASS.
- `R` PIC ensures non-crew persons get a CASS safety briefing.
- `R` Icing reported or forecast on route → no authorization, TKOF or continuation if **PIC** thinks safety may suffer, even if ACFT is equipped (605.30(a)).

#### Equipment — 702.42 / .44 / .45
<!-- id: law-702-equipment | level: CPL -->

- `R` Night: **≥1 landing light**; in icing, a means to illuminate or detect ice formation.
- `R` **Multi-engine in IMC**: **2 generators/alternators**, each on a separate engine (or rotor drive) + **2 independent energy sources**, ≥1 **not a battery**, each able to drive all flight instruments needing power, installed so one failure affects neither the other source nor the other instruments.
- `R` Pilot seat **and any seat beside it**: safety belt **with shoulder harness**.
- `R` External load: attachment device authorized in an **STC** or airworthiness approval for that configuration.

- Sources: [702.42](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.42.html), [702.44](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.44.html), [702.45](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.45.html).

#### Crew designation, validity, training, manuals, SOPs — 702.64 / .67 / .76 / .83 / .84
<!-- id: law-702-crew-validity-training-manuals-sop | level: CPL -->

- `R` Designate PIC (PIC + SIC if 2 pilots); record names, keep **≥180 days**.
- `R` **PPC valid to 1st day of 25th month**; competency check + annual training → **13th month**. Renewed in last 90 days → **+24 / +12 months**. Minister may extend **≤60 days**. Expired **≥24 months** → requalify per CASS.
- `R` Ground + flight training program, Minister-approved: indoctrination · upgrading · training for the aerial work · initial + annual (type, servicing/ground handling, emergency, surface contamination, persons carried on board or **externally**). Syllabus in COM; contamination awareness program for others.
- `R` COM parts + amendments to each crew member, ground ops and maintenance staff; or one copy **in each ACFT** (or, for parachute ops within **25 NM** of DEP, another suitable place) if COM has amendment procedures. Holders keep it current and accessible.
- `R` SOPs for each ACFT **required to have ≥2 pilots**, within AFM limits, CASS; copy **on board**.
- `T` 702 PPC **24 months** ≠ 703 PPC **12 months**.

- Sources: [702.64](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.64.html), [702.67](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.67.html), [702.76](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.76.html), [702.83](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.83.html), [702.84](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.84.html).

#### Flight time, FDP, rest, time free from duty — 702.91–.97
<!-- id: law-702-flight-time-fdp-rest | level: CPL -->

- `R` Monitoring system (flight time, FDP, rest) in COM; anyone aware of an exceedance → tell operator ASAP.
- `R` Max flight time: **1 200 h / 365 d · 300 h / 90 d · 120 h / 30 d (100 h on call) · 60 h / 7 d · single-pilot IFR 8 h / 24 h**. More only with AOC authorization + CASS. Limit reached → off flight duty until 702.93 rest or 702.96 time off (except 702.94).
- `R` Max FDP **14 consecutive h in 24 consecutive h**. Split: + **½ the rest, max +3 h**, with advance notice + **≥4 consecutive h** rest in suitable accommodation, uninterrupted; next rest + **≥ the extension**. Longer FDP only with AOC authorization + CASS.
- `R` Unforeseen operational circumstance **after FDP starts**: flight time and FDP may be exceeded if PIC, after consulting other flight crew, considers it safe + CASS met.
- `R` Delay **>3 h**, notified before leaving rest facility → FDP begins **3 h after original** report.
- `R` Time free: **24 consecutive h, 13× per 90 d and 3× per 30 d**; on call: **36 consecutive h / 7 d** or **3 consecutive days / 17 d** (start and duration notified). Other patterns only with AOC + CASS.
- `R` Positioning after FDP → extra rest **≥½** of travel beyond max FDP.

- Sources: [702.91](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.91.html), [702.92](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.92.html), [702.93](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.93.html), [702.94](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.94.html), [702.95](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.95.html), [702.96](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.96.html), [702.97](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-702.97.html).
