#### Definitions, operational control, crew designation — 604.01 / .25 / .26
<!-- id: law-604-definitions-operational-control | level: CPL -->

| Term | 604 meaning |
|---|---|
| Main base | personnel + ACFT + facilities, principal place of business |
| Sub-base | personnel + ACFT; operational control exercised there under the operator's system |
| Flight deck duty time | time at a flight crew position during flight time |
| Special authorization | Minister's authorization (604.05(2)) for a Div IV activity or a 604.74(1) requirement |
| Incorporated docs | "should"/"must" → "shall"; "operator" → "private operator"; "authority" → "Minister" |

- `R` Operational control system, sized to the operation, with procedures that ensure: 604 requirements met · W&B within AFM limits · **names of persons on board recorded before each flight** · SAR notified in time if overdue/missing.
- `R` Choose one dispatch model: **pilot self-dispatch** (flight planning, when crew reports DEP/ARR to operator, method to confirm safe ARR at unattended A/D in VFR or IFR FPL cancelled before LDG) **or co-authority dispatch** (adds flight following, flight watch, OFP approval/recording by PIC + dispatcher, PIC gets MET + OFP copy + can call dispatcher before TKOF).
- `R` Operational control docs kept **≥180 days** after the flight.
- `R` Designate PIC each flight; PIC + SIC if ≥2 flight crew; record names, keep **≥180 days**.

- Sources: [604.01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.01.html), [604.25](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.25.html), [604.26](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.26.html).

#### Checklist, aircraft operating manual, operational flight data sheet — 604.36 / .37 / .38
<!-- id: law-604-checklist-aom-flight-data-sheet | level: CPL -->

- `R` Checklist (602.60(1)(a)), or the part needed, given to **every crew member**; each crew member follows it.
- `R` Aircraft operating manual is **optional** (*may*). If made: procedures consistent with AFM · AFM performance data + limitations included and marked as AFM requirements **if AFM not on board** · operator SOPs, if any · identifies the ACFT.
- `R` No TKOF without an **operational flight data sheet**: date · nationality + registration marks · PIC name · DEP / DEST / ALTN (if any) A/D · estimated flight time · fuel endurance · fuel weight on board · zero fuel weight · TKOF weight + CG · persons on board · proposed DEP time · ETA.
- `R` After each flight PIC records flight time, DEP time, ARR time, ARR A/D. Operator keeps copy **≥180 days**.
- `T` 604 = operational flight **data sheet**; 702 / 703 = operational flight **plan** meeting Commercial Air Service Standards.

- Sources: [604.36](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.36.html), [604.37](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.37.html), [604.38](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.38.html).

#### Cabin safety, fuelling with passengers, safety features card — 604.82–.84, .86
<!-- id: law-604-cabin-safety-fuelling-safety-card | level: CPL -->

- `R` Before TKOF or surface movement with PAX: belts fastened (infants held, child restraints secured) · seat backs upright · chair tables + carry-on stowed · no unsuitable PAX in an emergency exit seat. Before LDG: PAX **directed** to do the same; move unsuitable exit-row PAX.
- `R` Emergency (time permitting): PIC directs the above + review safety features card + brace until ACFT stops + life preservers on if over water. Seat belt sign on in flight → belts + carry-on.
- `R` Reclined seat allowed for PAX certified by a physician unable to sit upright: not restricting evacuation · not next to / in front of an exit row · seat behind vacant.
- `R` Fuelling with PAX on board / boarding / deplaning (604.83), key conditions: two-way comm ground supervisor ↔ person on board trained in evacuation for the type · no GPU connect/disconnect, no ACFT combustion heater, no battery change or charger · no HF radio / WX radar unless AFM procedure · **suspend if lightning within 8 km** of the A/D · emergency lighting armed/on · no smoking signs on, no ignition source · **two exits** (one = boarding door) and their escape routes clear · person authorized to suspend fuelling on board · evacuation means at boarding door · boarding door open (or closed for climate only if crew on board, door not locked, inward-opening or fully openable without moving stairs).
- `R` Fuelling with an **engine running** + PAX (604.84): all 604.83 conditions + AFM allows that engine as an APU + **propeller brake set**.
- `R` Safety features card at each PAX seat **before** the briefing, type-specific: belts/harness · brace (each seat type, PAX with infant) · exits (incl. unusable in ditching) · oxygen (own mask first) · life preservers (adult, child ≥2, infant; when to inflate) · smoking · flotation / rafts. >19 PAX seats adds baggage stowage, seats/tables, floor path markings, route away from ACFT, floating attitude. No flight attendant required → card also shows location + access of emergency equipment.

- Sources: [604.82](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.82.html), [604.83](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.83.html), [604.84](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.84.html), [604.86](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.86.html).

#### Delayed reporting, time with no duties, controlled rest — 604.103 / .104 / .106
<!-- id: law-604-delayed-reporting-time-off-controlled-rest | level: CPL -->

- `R` Delay of reporting time **>3 h**, crew notified **within 12 h before** original report **and ≥1 h before** leaving the rest facility → FDP starts **3 h after original** reporting time.
- `R` Time with no assigned duties, one of: **≥36 consecutive h in 7 consecutive days** · or **≥3 consecutive calendar days in 17 consecutive days**.
- `R` Controlled rest on flight deck only with an operator program (guidelines, fatigue principles, procedures) + trained participants. PIC decides if conditions, flight length and crew state allow; briefs order, duration, wake-up triggers, control transfer, FA duties.
- `R` Supervising pilot: does the resting pilot's duties · **cruise only, done ≥30 min before top of descent** · **≤45 min** · resting pilot awake **≥15 min** before resuming duties (except abnormal/emergency) · gives operational briefing after. Both stay on the flight deck.
- `T` 700.52 delay rule differs (see `law-fatigue`).

- Sources: [604.103](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.103.html), [604.104](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.104.html), [604.106](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.106.html).

#### Maintenance authority, validity periods, manual distribution, SMS duties — 604.128 / .139 / .198 / .205
<!-- id: law-604-maintenance-validity-manual-sms | level: CPL -->

- `R` Maintenance or elementary work only by a person who has the 604.182(1) training (+ for elementary work: done it once supervised by an AME or approved training organization) **or** under a written agreement (tasks, conditions; operator responsible). Agreement kept **2 years**. Servicing staff meet 604.182(2) training.
- `R` Validity expires **1st day of the month after**: **37th** month → emergency procedures training (604.169(2), 604.179(z)(viii)–(ix)) and high-altitude indoctrination · **13th** month → all other training · **25th** month → competency check or PPC.
- `R` Renewed in the **last 90 days** → validity extended **36 / 12 / 24 months** respectively. Minister extends **≤60 days** if asked during validity and no reasonable chance to renew in those last 90 days.
- `R` Operations manual + every amendment to all personnel involved; holders keep it current and accessible on duty. Alternative: up-to-date parts kept **in each aircraft** instead of a crew copy.
- `R` SMS: personnel report to the operations manager any SMS issue (deficiencies, hazards, incidents, accidents) and share aviation-safety information with service partners.

- Sources: [604.128](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.128.html), [604.139](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.139.html), [604.198](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.198.html), [604.205](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-604.205.html).
