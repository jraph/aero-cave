#### Day VFR equipment — power-driven
<!-- id: law-day-vfr-equipment-power-driven | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

- Altimeter (`sensitive/adjustable` in controlled), ASI, independent magnetic direction, required engine/prop gauges, each main-tank fuel quantity, retractable-gear indication and required radio. `[CAR 605.14]`
- `T:` GPS/tablet does not replace required instruments.
***
#### Night VFR equipment — PAX / commercial
<!-- id: law-night-vfr-equipment-pax-commercial | from: 04 | also: law-night | level: PPL CPL -->

| CAVE | Rule / trap |
|---|---|
| `R` ALL night | `605.14(c–n)` day VFR EQPT + sensitive ALTM + turn/slip or turn coordinator¹ + electrical supply + spare fuses **≥50%** of accessible installed fuses **per rating**. [CAR 605.16(1)(a–e)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.16.html) |
| `R` NAV/light | Stabilized magnetic DG **if A/D not visible**; non-magnetic direction source **in NDA**; instrument illumination + position/anti-collision lights. [605.16(1)(f–k)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.16.html) |
| `R` PAX | **LDG light required when carrying PAX** under 605.16(1)(j); not exclusively commercial. [605.16(1)(j)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.16.html) |
| `R` commercial | Night VFR **Part VI Subpart 4 or Part VII Subparts 2–5** → **attitude indicator + VSI + ASI-system anti-ice means + OAT gauge** *in addition to 605.16(1)*; PAX **not** prerequisite. [605.16(4)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.16.html) |
| `R` 703 night | Air taxi ACFT at night → **≥1 LDG light**, whether PAX carried or not; in actual icing conditions → ice-formation illumination/detection means. [703.64(2)](https://www.laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-96-433/page-94.html) |
| `R` 703 SE PAX | **SE + PAX + IFR or night VFR prohibited UNLESS** AOC expressly authorized **AND** STD 723.22 complied with. [703.22](https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/page-93.html) |
| `R` 723 SE type | Eligible SE aeroplane: factory-built **turbine**, OR turbine + gearbox conversion under TC-approved **STC** (effective **SEP 1 2026**); prescribed ENG reliability + pilot training. **Piston SE not eligible under standard**. [STD 723.22(1)](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-723-air-taxi-aeroplanes-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) |
| `R` 723 SE EQPT | **2 independently powered attitude indicators + 2 independent generating sources**; auto/continuous ignition; ENG chip detector; radar ALTM; compliant manual throttle/fuel-control redundancy; emergency O₂; nearest-suitable-A/D electronic NAV; emergency electrical supply for optimal glide DESC; **airborne WX radar if TS reasonably expected** (effective APR 10 2026). [STD 723.22(2)](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-723-air-taxi-aeroplanes-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) |
| `T` | **Night VFR ≠ IFR EQPT**; `605.16(4)` commercial supplements **do not depend on PAX**; `703.22` SE PAX operation requires separate **AOC AUTH**, not merely extra EQPT. |

- `¹` Qualifying **third attitude indicator** may permit slip-skid indicator substitution under 605.16(2); check exact attitude capability.
- `M:` **Night=605.14(c–n)+ALTM+turn/slip+ELEC+spare fuses+DG/NDA conditional+instrument/ACFT lights | PAX→LDG light | COM VI-4/VII-2–5→AI+VSI+ASI anti-ice+OAT (PAX or not) | 703 night→LDG light+icing detect if icing | 703 SE PAX night→AOC AUTH+723.22 turbine/STC+special EQPT**.

#### IFR equipment
<!-- id: law-ifr-equipment | from: 04 | also: ifr | level: CPL IR -->

- IFR requires the applicable 605.16 equipment plus attitude indicator, VSI, OAT gauge and the remaining equipment/nav/comm items in 605.18.
- Equipment must be adequate for the route/procedure and serviceable.
- `[CAR 605.18]`
- `T:` “IFR-certified GPS installed” ≠ aircraft/procedure/current-database/RAIM/approval requirements all met.
***
#### Pebble
<!-- id: law-required-equipment-pebble | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- DAY VFR CAR 605.14 `altimeter (sensitive in controlled), ASI, magnetic direction, engine/prop gauges, fuel quantity each main tank, gear indication, required radio | GPS/tablet ≠ required instruments`
- NIGHT VFR CAR 605.16 `day equipment + sensitive altimeter + turn/slip + electrical supply + spare fuses ≥50% per rating + DG if aerodrome not visible + instrument and aircraft lights | passengers → landing light | commercial (VI-4, VII-2–5) → AI + VSI + ASI anti-ice + OAT, passengers or not`
- IFR CAR 605.18 `605.16 equipment + attitude indicator + VSI + OAT + nav/comm adequate for route | IFR GPS installed ≠ database/RAIM/approval met`
- 703 `SE + passengers + IFR or night VFR prohibited unless AOC authorized AND Std 723.22 (turbine) | night VFR ≠ IFR equipment`
