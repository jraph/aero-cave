#### Recency
<!-- id: law-recency | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

| Privilege | Requirement |
|---|---|
| general `5 y` | PIC/co-pilot in preceding `5 years`, or prescribed review + exam `[CAR 401.05(1)]` |
| recurrent `24 mo` | approved recurrent activity, recorded `[CAR 401.05(2); STD 421.05]` |
| passengers `6 mo` | `5 TO + 5 LDG`, same category/class; night flight requires all `5+5` at night `[CAR 401.05(2)(b)]` |
| instrument | `24 mo` check + `6 approaches / 6 mo` → canonical detail `ifr-instrument-rating-recency` [CAR 401.05(3)–(3.2)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-401.05.html) |

- `T:` medical, general, passenger and instrument recency are separate clocks; solo legality≠passenger legality.

#### Pebble
<!-- id: law-recency-pebble | level: PPL CPL -->

- RECENCY CAR 401.05 `5 y: PIC/co-pilot, or review + exam | 24 mo: recurrent activity | passengers: 5 TKOF + 5 LDG in 6 mo, same category/class; night passengers → all at night | instrument: 24 mo check + 6 approaches/6 mo | separate clocks; solo legality ≠ passenger legality`
- OPERATOR CREW `703/704: 3 TKOF + 3 LDG in 90 d | 705: 90 d, 3 TKOF/LDG + 1 sector OR 5 sectors OR qualifying training`
