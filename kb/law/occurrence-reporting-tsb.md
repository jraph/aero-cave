#### TSB occurrence reporting — AIM GEN 3.3–3.4
<!-- id: law-tsb-occurrence-reporting-aim-gen-3-3-3-4 | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

| CAVE | Rule / trap |
|---|---|
| `R` accident | Fatal/serious injury from ACFT operation; significant structural DMG affecting strength/performance/flight characteristics (specified minor/engine-only exclusions); missing/inaccessible ACFT. [AIM GEN 3.3](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/publications/transport-canada-aeronautical-information-manual-tc-aim) |
| `R` incident scope | Mandatory listed incidents for **MCTOW >2,250 kg OR ACFT under Part VII AOC**; **not** every private light-ACFT malfunction. |
| `R` examples | ENG failure/shutdown · fire/smoke · control difficulties · RWY excursion/gear-up · crew incapacitation · depressurization/emerg DESC · fuel divert/priority · wrong fuel · collision/SEP loss · emergency priority · dropped slung load · dangerous goods release. |
| `R` who/when | Owner/operator/PIC/crew/ATS direct knowledge → TSB **ASAP by quickest means**; remaining info **as available ≤30d**. |
| `R` evidence | Preserve; safety measures permitted, document/advise TSB as practicable. |
| `T` | Accident duty ≠ incident threshold; **2,250 kg** applies listed incident class, not accidents. |

`M:` **TSB accident: fatal/serious/structural DMG/missing | incident >2,250kg OR Part VII | ASAP fastest; balance ≤30d | preserve evidence**.
