#### Operating instructions, operational control, authorization, OFP, maintenance — 703.14 / .16–.19
<!-- id: law-703-operational-control-flight-plan | level: CPL -->

- `R` Ops personnel instructed on duties and follow the COM.
- `R` Operational control system meeting CASS, **under the control of the operations manager**.
- `R` Flight starts only when authorized per COM. OFP (CASS) prepared per COM; PIC leaves a copy **at the point of departure** per COM; operator keeps OFP + amendments **≥180 days**.
- `R` No TKOF in an ACFT not maintained under the operator's **maintenance control system**.

- Sources: [703.14](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.14.html), [703.16](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.16.html), [703.17](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.17.html), [703.18](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.18.html), [703.19](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.19.html).

#### Flight operations — 703.23–.29 / .34 / .37 / .42
<!-- id: law-703-flight-operations | level: CPL -->

- `R` Land ACFT over water beyond gliding reach of shore (except TKOF/LDG) → AOC authorization + CASS.
- `R` Single-engined ACFT with **>9 passengers** only if transport category **helicopter** + AOC + CASS.
- `R` External load with passengers only if the load carriage is authorized in a **TC or STC**.
- `R` No simulated emergencies affecting flight characteristics with **passengers or cargo** on board.
- `R` VFR obstacle clearance (except TKOF/LDG): **night ≥1 000 ft above highest obstacle within 3 mi of route** · **day, aeroplane ≥300 ft AGL and ≥300 ft horizontally from any obstacle**.
- `R` Day VFR, uncontrolled, <1 000 ft AGL: aeroplane <**2 SM**, helicopter <**1 SM** flight visibility only with AOC + CASS.
- `R` Start VFR only if current reports and forecasts, if obtainable, show VFR along route **and at destination**.
- `R` IFR or **night VFR** in uncontrolled airspace off an air route → route established by operator per CASS.
- `R` W&B within AFM limits **in every phase**; W&B system (CASS) described in COM with form-preparation instructions.
- `R` Icing reported/forecast → PIC's safety judgment overrides being equipped (605.30(a)).

- Sources: [703.23](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.23.html), [703.24](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.24.html), [703.25](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.25.html), [703.26](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.26.html), [703.27](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.27.html), [703.28](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.28.html), [703.29](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.29.html), [703.34](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.34.html), [703.37](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.37.html), [703.42](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.42.html).

#### Single-pilot IMC equipment, shoulder harness — 703.66 / .69
<!-- id: law-703-equipment-single-pilot-imc-harness | level: CPL -->

- `R` **Single-pilot IMC**: **autopilot** able to hold flight and manoeuvre about lateral + longitudinal axes · **headset with boom mic** (or equivalent) + **transmit button on control column** · **chart holder** easily readable + lit.
- `R` Pilot seat and any seat beside it: safety belt **with shoulder harness**.
- `T` Autopilot requirement = **single-pilot in IMC** only; not VMC, not two-pilot.

- Sources: [703.66](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.66.html), [703.69](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.69.html).

#### Crew designation, validity, training, manuals, SOPs — 703.87 / .91 / .98 / .106 / .107
<!-- id: law-703-crew-validity-training-manuals-sop | level: CPL -->

- `R` Designate PIC (PIC + SIC if 2 pilots); record names, keep **≥180 days**.
- `R` **PPC, competency check and annual training → 1st day of 13th month**. Renewed in last 90 days → **+12 months**. Minister may extend **≤60 days**. Expired **≥24 months** → requalify per CASS.
- `R` Training program (Minister-approved): indoctrination · upgrading · initial + annual (type, servicing/ground handling, emergency, **operational control personnel**, surface contamination) · **seaplane pilots: underwater egress initially then every 3 years**. Syllabus in COM; contamination awareness for others.
- `R` Fatigue management training: personal strategies (sleep hygiene, lifestyle/exercise/diet, alcohol and drugs) · fatigue impact on safety · sleep science · causes/consequences · recognizing fatigue in self and others · sleep disorders · human/organizational factors (sleep, shift work, overtime, circadian rhythm, time zones).
- `R` COM parts + amendments to crew, ground ops and maintenance staff; or copy **in each ACFT** if COM has amendment procedures.
- `R` SOPs for each ACFT **required to have ≥2 pilots**; copy on board.

- Sources: [703.87](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.87.html), [703.91](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.91.html), [703.98](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.98.html), [703.106](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.106.html), [703.107](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-703.107.html).

#### Maintenance arrangements, elementary work, servicing — 706.09–.11
<!-- id: law-706-maintenance-arrangements-elementary-work-servicing | level: CPL -->

- `R` Maintenance only by a person/organization with adequate facilities, equipment, parts and staff on site **and**: AMO certificate (573.02) rated for the work · or abroad, approved by a state with a recognition agreement with Canada · or Minister-approved.
- `R` Arrangement specifies the maintenance and tasks, follows the MCM procedures (or Minister-approved). Abroad without AMO → Minister issues a **maintenance specification** when the agreement/foreign state requires it or no agreement exists. Operator ensures all tasks are completed.
- `R` **Elementary work** unsupervised only after training under the 706.12 program **and** having done that task before under **direct supervision** of an AME or approved training organization.
- `R` Anyone performing **or requesting** servicing has completed 706.12 training for it.
- `T` Elementary work ≠ servicing: elementary needs prior supervised performance; servicing needs training only.

- Sources: [706.09](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-706.09.html), [706.10](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-706.10.html), [706.11](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-706.11.html).
