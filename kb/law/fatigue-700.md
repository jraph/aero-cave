#### PEDs, Division III scope, records, home base, meals — 700.12 / .19 / .20 / .36 / .37
<!-- id: law-700-ped-scope-records-home-base-meals | level: CPL -->

- `R` PEDs (703 / 704 / 705): despite 602.08(1), operator **may** permit a PED once it has **validated** no adverse effect on ACFT operation or systems in any phase of flight.
- `R` Division III fatigue rules **do not apply** to 702 (aerial work) operations or to **medevac** flights (Division IV instead).
- `R` Times of day: acclimatized → local time where the crew member is; not acclimatized → local time at the last place they were acclimatized.
- `R` Monitoring system for flight time, FDP, hours of work, rest, described in the COM. Records per crew member: flight times · start/end/duration of each FDP, duty period and rest · time free from duty · PIC notices under 700.63(4). Keep **24 months**.
- `R` Every flight crew member gets an assigned **home base**.
- `R` Meal break: **≥15 min every 6 h** of FDP to eat and drink.

- Sources: [700.12](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.12.html), [700.19](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.19.html), [700.20](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.20.html), [700.36](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.36.html), [700.37](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.37.html).

#### Disruptive schedules, time-zone rest, positioning, consecutive night FDPs — 700.41 / .42 / .43 / .51
<!-- id: law-700-disruptive-schedules-time-zones-positioning-nights | level: CPL -->

- `R` Disruptive schedule: on top of 700.40 rest, **one local night's rest** between late/night duty → following early duty, and between early duty → following late/night duty. Not required where local time differs **>4 h** from the last acclimatized location.
- `R` FDP ends **away from home base**, start vs end local time differs **4 h** → **11 consecutive h** rest; **>4 h** → **14 consecutive h**, in suitable accommodation.
- `R` FDP starts in another time zone and **ends at home base**:

| Difference start vs home | Condition | Rest before next FDP |
|---|---|---|
| 4 h | away >36 consecutive h | 13 consecutive h |
| >4 to ≤10 h | away ≤60 h, no FDP in window of circadian low | 1 local night |
| >4 to ≤10 h | away >60 h, or FDP touches WOCL | 2 local nights |
| >10 h | away ≤60 h | 2 local nights |
| >10 h | away >60 h | 3 local nights |

- `R` Positioning right after an FDP that, with travel, exceeds the 700.28 max FDP → rest = **hours of work** (excess ≤3 h) or **hours of work + excess** (excess >3 h); never less than 700.40(1). Excess >3 h only if crew **agrees** and excess ≤**7 h**. Positioning not followed by an FDP counts as FDP for 700.40 rest.
- `R` **≤3 consecutive FDPs** touching **02:00–05:59**, then one local night's rest. Up to **5** if each FDP has **3 h rest in suitable accommodation** + **56 consecutive h free from duty** after the last.

- Sources: [700.41](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.41.html), [700.42](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.42.html), [700.43](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.43.html), [700.51](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.51.html).

#### Delayed reporting, standby, controlled rest — 700.52 / .71 / .72
<!-- id: law-700-delayed-reporting-standby-controlled-rest | level: CPL -->

- `R` Delay advised **before leaving suitable accommodation** → FDP length measured from initial or delayed report, **whichever gives the shorter max FDP**.
- `R` FDP begins: delay **<4 h** → at delayed time · **4 h to <10 h** → **4 h after initial** report · **≥10 h** → delay counts as **rest**, if advised before leaving and rest not disturbed before an agreed time.
- `R` No agreed contact time → operator may disturb only in the **30 min** before scheduled departure from accommodation or the **60 min** before initial report.
- `R` Standby: place sheltered from elements, seating, food and drink, ideally not public. Standby with no flight duty → rest **12 h, or 11 h + travel, or 10 h in operator accommodation** at home base; **10 h** away.
- `R` Controlled rest: **≤45 min**, cruise only, done **≥30 min before scheduled descent** · one pilot rests at a time · **≥2 flight crew stay on the flight deck**. Before: transfer duties, review flight status + wake-up criteria, tell FAs start/end. No duties until **15 min after** rest ends; operational briefing on return.
- `T` Delay: 604.103 / 702.95 / 700.121 = **>3 h → FDP starts original +3 h** ≠ 700.52 **4 h / 10 h** bands.

- Sources: [700.52](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.52.html), [700.71](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.71.html), [700.72](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.72.html).

#### Medevac (Division IV): scope, fitness, flight time, FDP — 700.100–.104
<!-- id: law-700-medevac-scope-flight-time-fdp | level: CPL -->

- `R` Applies to air operators and crew on **medical evacuation flights**; positioning before/after a medevac counts as medevac. Not 702 operations.
- `R` Monitoring system (flight time, FDP, rest) in the COM. Anyone aware an assignment would break max flight time or FDP → tell operator ASAP.
- `R` Fitness: crew says before FDP they are too fatigued → operator **shall not** let FDP start. Fatigued during FDP → tell other crew + operator ASAP; single pilot in flight → tell operator ASAP after landing. Missed rest or time free → report ASAP.
- `R` Max flight time (includes other flying + full time of augmented flights):

| Window | Limit |
|---|---|
| 7 consecutive days | **40 h** (704 / 705, aeroplane) · **60 h** (703, or any helicopter) |
| 30 consecutive days | **120 h** · **100 h** if on call |
| 90 consecutive days | **300 h** |
| 365 consecutive days | **1 200 h** |
| Single-pilot | **8 h in 24 consecutive h** |

- `R` Max FDP **14 h**.

- Sources: [700.100](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.100.html), [700.101](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.101.html), [700.102](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.102.html), [700.103](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.103.html), [700.104](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.104.html).

#### Medevac rest, split duty, time free, delays, extensions, reserve — 700.116–.121 / .133 / .134
<!-- id: law-700-medevac-rest-split-time-off-reserve | level: CPL -->

- `R` Rest after FDP = **10 h + travel** to and from the rest place; advance notice of rest and its length.
- `R` Positioning after FDP → extra rest **≥½** of travel time beyond max FDP.
- `R` Split duty: FDP may exceed 14 h by **½ the rest, max +3 h**, if notice given before FDP + **≥4 consecutive h** rest in suitable accommodation + not interrupted. Following rest extended by **≥ the extension**.
- `R` Time free from duty, one of: **36 consecutive h / 7 d** · **3 consecutive days / 17 d** · **4 consecutive days / 19 d**; advance notice.
- `R` **3 consecutive FDPs >12 h** → **≥24 consecutive h** free from flight duty after, unless ≥24 h free between each.
- `R` Delay **>3 h**, advised before leaving the rest facility → FDP starts **3 h after original** report.
- `R` Unforeseen operational circumstances: PIC, after consulting **all crew** on fatigue, may extend max flight time **and** FDP by **≤3 h**, notifying operator of length + reason; next rest increased by ≥ the FDP extension. Notices kept **24 months**.
- `R` Reserve: **≥10 consecutive h rest in 24 h** if 24 h notice, or ≥10 h notice and no duty in those 10 h, or no FDP and no interruption **22:00–06:00**. Next rest start shifts **≤3 h** from previous, **≤8 h total in 7 days**. Rest not given + called or reporting 22:00–06:00 → max FDP **10 h**, next rest + **≥½** of that FDP.
- `T` Medevac: air taxi (703) gets the **higher** 7-day limit (60 h), not the lower. Medevac 14 h fixed FDP + 40/60 h per 7 d ≠ Division III 700.28 matrix + 700.27 limits.

- Sources: [700.116](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.116.html), [700.117](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.117.html), [700.118](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.118.html), [700.119](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.119.html), [700.120](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.120.html), [700.121](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.121.html), [700.133](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.133.html), [700.134](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-700.134.html).
