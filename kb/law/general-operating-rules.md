# General operating rules / Règles générales d’utilisation
<!-- concept: law-oprules | level: PPL CPL -->

[Index](README.md) · concept `law-oprules` · unit IDs are stable: never rename one.

***
#### PIC responsibility / emergency
<!-- id: law-pic-responsibility-emergency | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

- PIC is responsible; reckless/negligent operation is prohibited. `[CAR 602.01.1–602.01.2]`
- Emergency deviation only as necessary for safety. `[CAR 602.01.3]` `M:` authority≠absence of accountability.
***
#### Aircraft icing / clean aircraft
<!-- id: law-aircraft-icing-clean-aircraft | from: 04 | also: deice | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- No takeoff with frost, ice or snow adhering to critical surfaces. `[CAR 602.11]`
- When contamination may reasonably be expected, perform the required immediate pre-takeoff contamination inspection. `[CAR 602.11]`
- `M:` clean-wing concept: no “little bit is fine.”
***
#### Pre-flight information
<!-- id: law-pre-flight-information | from: 04 | also: law-plan | level: PPL CPL -->

- PIC must be familiar with available information appropriate to intended flight. `[CAR 602.71]`
- For flight away from aerodrome vicinity: includes weather, fuel, alternatives, runway/airport suitability, delays and relevant operational information.
- `M:` `WX-NOTAM-FUEL-W&B-PERF-ROUTE-AIRSPACE-RUNWAY-ALTERNATES`.
***
#### Baggage / cargo
<!-- id: law-baggage-cargo | from: 04 | also: agk-wb | level: PPL CPL -->

- Carry-on baggage, equipment and cargo must be stowed/secured so they cannot shift, obstruct controls/exits or injure occupants. `[CAR 602.86]`
- Respect W&B, baggage-area structural limits and dangerous-goods rules.
- `T:` within total weight ≠ correctly secured or within CG.
***
#### Passenger briefing
<!-- id: law-passenger-briefing | from: 01, 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

- Before takeoff brief exits, seat belts/harnesses/restraints, seats/tables, baggage, oxygen where applicable and smoking restrictions. `[CAR 602.89]`
- Where applicable include life preservers, first-aid/survival equipment, ELT and raft information.
- `M:` brief normal use + emergency action, not just “seat belt on.”
- Pax brief memory: belts/doors/exits/no smoking/ELT/survival/sterile cockpit/emerg. `M:` SAFETY.
***
#### Safety belts / restraints
<!-- id: law-safety-belts-restraints | from: 04 | also: law-elt | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- Seats, safety belts and restraint systems must meet 605.22–605.28 requirements.
- PIC ensures passengers are instructed and comply during required phases/conditions.
- `T:` one belt for two persons is not the default; child-restraint provisions are specific.
***
#### Right-of-way / collision avoidance
<!-- id: law-right-of-way-collision-avoidance | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

- Distress has priority; ROW never removes collision-avoidance duty. `[CAR 602.19; 602.21]`
- Converging: traffic on right has ROW; head-on: both right; overtaking: pass/keep clear right.
- Powered aeroplane gives way to airship/glider/balloon/towing aircraft; landing aircraft has ROW, but lower aircraft may not cut in on final.
- `M:` `RIGHT-RIGHT-RIGHT`. `T:` ATC clearance does not remove see-and-avoid in VMC.
***
#### Minimum altitudes / distances
<!-- id: law-minimum-altitudes-distances | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

- Built-up area/open-air assembly: altitude allowing emergency landing without hazard and aeroplane at least `1000 ft` above highest obstacle within `2000 ft` horizontally. `[CAR 602.14]`
- Elsewhere: at least `500 ft` from any person, vessel, vehicle or structure. `[CAR 602.14]`
- Exceptions include takeoff/approach/landing and authorized low-altitude operations under 602.15.
- `T:` “500 AGL” is not the wording; it is `500 ft from` persons/vessels/vehicles/structures.
***
#### Aerobatic manoeuvres
<!-- id: law-aerobatic-manoeuvres | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

- Restrictions include designated airspace/areas, minimum visibility, cloud clearance, altitude and passenger conditions. `[CAR 602.27–602.28]`
- Aircraft POH/category/W&B limitations also apply.
- `T:` POH-approved spin ≠ legal anywhere/any altitude/with passengers.

#### Portable electronic devices — CAR 602.08
<!-- id: law-ped-car-602-08 | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- Operator shall not permit PED use if it could adversely affect aircraft operation/systems. [CAR 602.08(1)]
- No person uses a PED on board without operator permission. [CAR 602.08(2)]
- `T:` rule is "could affect" + operator permission, not a list of banned devices. EFB = operator-approved use.
***
#### Fuel dumping — 602.30
<!-- id: law-fuel-dumping-602-30 | level: CPL -->

- `R` Jettison fuel in flight only if **necessary for aviation safety** and all appropriate measures taken to minimize danger to life and environmental damage, as circumstances permit.

- Sources: [602.30](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-602.30.html).

#### Special flight operations certificates — 603.01 / .36 / .37 / .65 / .66
<!-- id: law-special-flight-operations-603 | level: CPL -->

- `R` **Special aviation event** (air show etc., **not a fly-in**) → special flight operations certificate (SFOC) — special aviation event.
- `R` **Parachute descent** in/into controlled airspace or an air route, or over/into a built-up area or open-air assembly → SFOC — parachuting (otherwise prohibited by 602.26).
- `R` Other operations not under Part VII needing an SFOC: TKOF/LDG in a **built-up area** away from an airport/heliport/military A/D (not balloons) · aerial application, inspection or photography below 602.14(2)(a) distances · heli Class B/C/D external loads over built-up area or assembly · persons entering or leaving a powered ACFT in flight (except parachuting) · **aerobatics below 2 000 ft AGL**.

- Sources: [603.01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-603.01.html), [603.36](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-603.36.html), [603.37](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-603.37.html), [603.65](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-603.65.html), [603.66](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-603.66.html), [602.26](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-602.26.html).
