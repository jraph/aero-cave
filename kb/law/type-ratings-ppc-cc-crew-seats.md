#### ACFT type ratings · HP / two-crew / IATRA / PPC — Oct 2026
<!-- id: law-acft-type-ratings-hp-two-crew-iatra-ppc-oct-2026 | from: 04 | level: CPL -->

| CAVE | Rule / trap |
|---|---|
| `R` Blanket | Single-pilot **non-high-performance** aeroplanes → blanket rating with applicable class; otherwise individual type rating where required. |
| `R` HP test | **Single-pilot** aeroplane per minimum-crew document AND **VNE ≥250 KIAS OR VSO ≥80 KIAS**; **OR** amateur-built aeroplane wing loading above AWM 549.103 threshold. [CAR 400.01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-400.01.html) |
| `T` Complex ≠ HP | Complex = flaps + constant-speed PROP + retractable gear (except seaplane); complex, turbine-powered or pressurized ≠ automatically HP. |
| `R` HP individual TYPE | **STD 421.40(3)(c):** type ground training + type FLT training + **≥200 h aeroplane pilot FLT time** + **qualifying FLT in 12 mo BEFORE rating application** supervised by TC Inspector or qualified person under 425.21(7)(a); **IATRA/PPC not stated as initial HP requirement**. |
| `R` Two-crew individual TYPE | **Minimum crew ≥2 pilots** per type approval → type ground+FLT training + **≥250 h aeroplane pilot FLT time**, **OR** approved integrated ATP(A) course alternative (STD 421.40, effective 17 DEC 2025). |
| `R` Two-crew knowledge | First PPL(A)/CPL(A) two-crew endorsement: **IATRA ≥70% within 24 mo** before application **OR SAMRA+SARON ≥70% each within 24 mo**; ATPL(A) holder meets knowledge; approved integrated ATP(A): ATP exams within **5 years** before application. |
| `R` Two-crew skill | Type-specific **PPC within 12 mo before application** (Part VII / Minister-accepted); **approved AQP LOE** accepted; CAR 604 certificate holder: approved training/proficiency programme + **PPC or PCC under 604.143 within 12 mo**. [STD 421.40](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-421-flight-crew-permits-licences-ratings-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) |
| `T` Three clocks | **IATRA 24 mo** = exam-to-first-rating application; **qualifying FLT/PPC 12 mo** = test-to-type-rating application; **operational PPC currency** = independent recurrent check, **not TYPE rating expiration**. |
| `R` CAR 704 PPC transition | **Until 01 NOV 2026:** qualifying new PPC may use **24-mo exemption**; **on/after 01 NOV 2026:** no new 24-mo PPC; **standard 12 mo**. Existing PPC issued under 24-mo exemption keeps original validity per TC Bulletin 01/26. [TC ACP/AQPE Bulletin 01/26](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/commercial-air-services/approved-check-pilot-acp-advanced-qualification-program-aqp/acp-aqpe-bulletins/approved-check-pilot-acp-advanced-qualification-program-aqp-evaluator-bulletin-no-01-26) |
| `T` Operations | TYPE endorsement ≠ operator authorization, PPC, IR, recurrent training or flight crew recency. ATR/other two-crew turboprop uses two-crew path, not HP-speed shortcut. |

**604 scope vs registration / Champ 604 et enregistrement :** §604.02 applies when **ANY ONE** of the four aircraft characteristics above is present. Separately, §604.03 generally requires a private-operator registration document to carry passengers or goods in a **large aeroplane**, a **turbojet aeroplane**, or a **turbine-powered pressurized aeroplane certificated for more than six passenger seats** (Part VII / flight-permit exceptions). A small, non-pressurized piston twin meets §604.02 solely because it is multi-engine; that fact alone does not trigger §604.03 registration. / §604.02 : **un seul** des quatre critères suffit. §604.03 : enregistrement généralement requis pour transporter passagers ou marchandises en **gros avion**, **avion à turboréacteur**, ou **avion à turbine pressurisé homologué pour plus de six sièges passagers** (exceptions partie VII / permis de vol). Un petit bimoteur à pistons non pressurisé relève du §604.02 par ses moteurs, sans que cela déclenche seul l'enregistrement §604.03.

**Aircraft examples / Exemples d'avions:** Illustrations only, not assigned CAR categories: operator certificate/authorization, certified passenger seating and mission control applicability. PA-30 private multi-engine, PA-46 Malibu private pressurized piston, C208 private turbine, CJ3 private jet **or** 704 authorized turbojet; DHC-6 19-seat commuter or fewer-seat variants/other authorized operations. / Exemples indicatifs seulement : certificat d'exploitation, capacité passagers homologuée et mission prévalent; un même type peut relever de plusieurs sous-parties selon l'exploitation.

**Aircraft classification / Classement des aéronefs:** MCTOW = maximum certificated takeoff weight / masse maximale homologuée au décollage (MMHD). MZFW = maximum zero fuel weight / masse maximale sans carburant. **704 turbojet uses MZFW, not MCTOW.** Seating limits exclude pilot seats except where the rule expressly uses type-certificate passenger authorization; 702 aerial work and 604 private operations are defined by purpose/equipment, not one shared seat cut-off. §604.02 applicability is broader than §604.03 registration. Ministerial 703/704/705 authorizations and helicopter-specific conditions may apply. / **704 turboréacteur : masse maxi sans carburant, non masse au décollage.**

#### 703 single-pilot IFR with passengers / IFR monopilote avec passagers — STD 723.86
<!-- id: q17-703-single-pilot-ifr-passengers-ifr-monopilo | from: 17 | also: law-commercial | level: CPL IR -->

**Authorized exception to two-pilot IFR / Dispense autorisée de copilote IFR.** In addition to 703 ME PIC **PPC 12 mo**, the PIC needs **1,000 h total**, **100 h ME if operating ME**, **50 h simulated or actual flight in IMC**, **50 h on aeroplane type**. PPC must assess single-pilot procedures including autopilot; for pressurized aeroplanes, **FL250 maximum**. These thresholds do **not** apply to every 703 flight. / En plus du **PPC CDB multimoteur 12 mois** : **1 000 h total**, **100 h multimoteur si appareil ME**, **50 h de vol en IMC réel ou simulé**, **50 h sur type**; PPC monopilote et procédures pilote automatique; avion pressurisé **FL250 max**. **Pas** un minimum universel pour tous les vols 703.

**M:** `703 SP-IFR PAX = 1000 TT / 100 ME / 50 IMC / 50 TYPE + PPC`. [CASS 723.86](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-723-air-taxi-aeroplanes-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars)
