#### Maintenance approvals / abnormal occurrences
<!-- id: law-maintenance-approvals-abnormal-occurrences | from: 04 | also: law-report | level: PPL CPL -->

| CAVE | Recall / rule |
|---|---|
| `R` AME | Aircraft Maintenance Engineer = licensed person; maintenance-release privileges depend on licence, rating, work and CAR 571 authority. [CAR 403](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-403.01.html) · [571.11](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-571.11.html) |
| `R` AMO | Approved Maintenance Organization = TC-approved organization/scope; **not required for every private-ACFT inspection**. [CAR 573](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-573.02.html) |
| `R` abnormal occurrence | Hard/overweight LDG, prop strike, excessive loads/turbulence, lightning, wind/jet blast suspected damage → applicable inspection per MFR + STD 625 App G **before return to service**; release by appropriately authorized person/org. [STD 625 App G](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-625-aircraft-equipment-maintenance-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) |
| `R` PRM | **Person Responsible for Maintenance** → AMO appoints; oversees QA/SMS corrective actions; accountable even when management functions assigned. [573.03](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-573.03.html) · [573.04](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-573.04.html) |
| `R` PRM QUAL | MAINT performance/direct supervision **≥6 yr**, incl. **≥6 mo in preceding 2 yr**; CAR open-book **≥70%** (AME licence/legacy exemptions for test; limited AMO rating/risk-assessment exception for experience). [STD 573.04(1)](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-573-approved-maintenance-organizations-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) · [573.03(4)–(5)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-573.03.html) |
| `R` PRM ACK | Sign acceptance to TC **≤30 days** after appointment; delegation of specified functions in **MPM ≠ transfer of responsibility**. [573.04(1),(4)–(6)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-573.04.html) |
| `R` PRMCS | **Person Responsible for Maintenance Control System** → AOC holder appoints employee; **≥70% open-book** unless AME licence or legacy exemption; ensures MAINT control / removes unsafe or non-compliant ACFT from operation. [706.03](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-706.03.html) |
| `T` PRM/PRMCS | **AMO→PRM · Air operator→PRMCS**; if same holder holds **AOC + AMO**, **same person** fills both roles [706.03(7)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-706.03.html). PRM/PRMCS appointment **≠ AME MAINT-release privileges**. |
| `R` MPM | Maintenance Policy Manual → AMO procedures. [573.10](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-573.10.html) |
| `R` MCM | Maintenance Control Manual → applicable air-operator maintenance-control procedures. [706.08](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-706.08.html) |
| `R` MSA | Maintenance Schedule Approval → where required, approved schedule/intervals; **not universal to every private ACFT**. [605.86](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.86.html) |
| `R` DML | Deferred Maintenance List → defect tracking (where used); **DML ≠ MEL authority**. [605.09–.10](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.10.html) |
| `T` | AME≠AMO · INSP requirement≠mandatory AMO · defect entry≠maintenance release. |

`M:` **AME=licenced individual | AMO=approved org | AMO→PRM (6 yr/6 mo, test ≥70% unless exempt, TC ACK ≤30d) | AOC→PRMCS (test ≥70% unless exempt) | dual AOC+AMO→same person | MPM AMO | MCM operator | MSA intervals | DML defects | App G→INSP**.

#### Core aircraft documents
<!-- id: law-core-aircraft-documents | from: 01, 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

| Document | Rule |
|---|---|
| Certificate of Registration | carried on board `[CAR 202.26]` |
| Flight authority / C of A / permit | in effect, conditions complied with, normally carried `[CAR 605.03]` |
| AFM/POH if required | current applicable amendments/supplements available to crew `[CAR 605.04]` |
| Journey log | carried unless a listed exception applies `[CAR 605.95]` |
| Radio licence | carried if one has been issued for onboard apparatus `[CAR 605.03.1]` |

- Journey-log exception: may remain behind when no landing and shutdown is planned anywhere other than departure, subject to exact CAR conditions.
- `T:` proof of insurance is required by CAR 606.02, but “must be carried on board” is not stated as a universal CAR 202/605 document rule.
- PIC responsible. Also onboard as applicable: required licences/medical.
- `T:` valid document≠current pilot or airworthy aircraft. Current CFS/AIP/NOTAM/POH wins.

#### ACFT REG / airworthiness — CoR · CoA
<!-- id: law-acft-reg-airworthiness-cor-coa | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

| CAVE | Rule / trap |
|---|---|
| `R` CoR | Certificate of Registration; carry onboard; registered custody/control **≠ proof of title**. [202.26](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-202.26.html) |
| `R` CoR fields | **REG marks · ACFT manufacturer/model/serial no. · registered owner name/address · purpose PVT/COM/State · issuing authority/date**; `purpose` = statistical designation **≠ operating AUTH or MAINT category**. [TC AC 202-002 §3, 7.2](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/reference-centre/advisory-circulars/advisory-circular-ac-no-202-002) |
| `T` CoR vs register | **MTOW stored in Canadian Civil Aircraft Register (CCAR), NOT displayed on CoR**; CCAR MTOW ≠ approved AFM/POH limit. [TC AC 202-002 §7.2(3)](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/reference-centre/advisory-circulars/advisory-circular-ac-no-202-002) |
| `R` transfer | Any transfer of legal custody/control → CoR cancelled; former registered owner informs TC **in writing ≤7 days**; Part 203 leasing provisions may apply. [202.35](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-202.35.html) |
| `R` interim | First qualifying transfer to eligible new owner → deemed interim REG; **no second successive interim transfer**; ends at earliest trigger, including **3 mo**, new CoR or further transfer. [202.36](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-202.36.html) |
| `R` temporary | TC-issued temporary CoR for registration processing; **≤3 mo** or earlier termination. [202.25](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-202.25.html) |
| `R` provisional | Movement/import under **specific route/dates/conditions**, not general 3-mo authority. [202.37](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-202.37.html) |
| `R` CoA | Certificate of Airworthiness = flight authority **≠ CoR**; transfer alone does not cancel CoA if validity/airworthiness conditions continue. [507.11](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-507.11.html) |
| `R` special | Special CoA ≠ Flight Permit; see FLT-authority classification table below; conditions control. [CAR 507.03–.04](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/FullText.html) |
| `T` | REG≠title≠airworthiness · CoA alone cannot replace REG · dual-state REG prohibited. |

`M:` **CoR: REG marks + manufacturer/model/serial + owner name/address + purpose PVT/COM/State + issue info | MTOW NOT printed | transfer→cancel + TC ≤7d | interim ≤3mo/2nd NO | TEMP ≤3mo | PROV route-specific | CoA independent**.

#### Flight authorities — CAR 507
<!-- id: law-flight-authorities-car-507 | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

| Flight authority | Classification / meaning |
|---|---|
| `C of A` (standard) | Aircraft conforms to approved, non-restricted type design and is safe for FLT. |
| `Special C of A — Provisional` | Provisional type certificate. |
| `Special C of A — Restricted` | Restricted-category type certificate or approved modified/special-purpose ACFT. |
| `Special C of A — Amateur-built` | Built to amateur-built airworthiness requirements (AWM 549). |
| `Special C of A — Limited` | Eligible aircraft accepted under Limited classification (e.g. certain historic/ex-military ACFT). |
| `Special C of A — Owner-maintenance` | Eligible ACFT with owner-maintenance privileges; recreational purposes only. |
| `Flight Permit — Experimental` | Aeronautical R&D or demonstration of compliance with airworthiness standards; not the Canadian amateur-built classification. |
| `Flight Permit — Specific Purpose` | Defined flight of nonconforming but safe-to-fly ACFT when no CoA/Special CoA is in effect (e.g. ferry). |

- `T:` Amateur-built ≠ Experimental in Canada; Normal/Utility/Aerobatic/Commuter/Transport = **type-certification categories**, not FLT-authority classifications.
- `T PAX:` Classification alone ≠ passenger authorization; check FLT-authority conditions, operating limits, pilot privileges and applicable CARs.
- `SRC:` [CAR 507.02–.04, .09–.11](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/FullText.html) · [AWM Standard 507.02–.04](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-507-flight-authority-certificate-noise-compliance-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars).

#### AAIR — Annual Airworthiness Information Report · CAR 501
<!-- id: law-aair-annual-airworthiness-information-report-car | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

| CAVE | Rule / trap |
|---|---|
| `R` WHO | Canadian ACFT owner → AAIR to TC; exemptions: ultra-light; RPAS without issued/applied design approval document; reported out-of-service ACFT for ≥1 reporting period, with **TC notified forthwith on return to service**. [CAR 501.01](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-501.01.html) |
| `R` WHEN | **JAN 1–DEC 31** reporting period → submit **≤MAR 30 following year**, or **different date agreed beforehand with TC**. [CAR 501.03](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-501.03.html) |
| `R` HOW | Individual via **CAWIS** or **Form 24-0059** (mail/fax); approved consolidated fleet report permitted. [STD 501.01](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-501-annual-airworthiness-information-report-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) |
| `R` DATA | REG marks · total/annual **air time** · aircraft make/model/serial · base/region · REG & flight-authority type · type certificate · empty W&B/MCTOW/config · **ENG + PROP make/model/serial if applicable** · skis/floats if applicable · significant DMG/repair certification · owner signature/CAWIS authentication. [STD 501.02](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-501-annual-airworthiness-information-report-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) |
| `R` INSP | **Except Part IV/VII ACFT**: last annual/equivalent INSP date + certifying AME licence no. or AMO approval no.; owner-maintenance/amateur-built special CoA → inspector name. [STD 501.02(1)(c)](https://tc.canada.ca/en/corporate-services/acts-regulations/list-regulations/canadian-aviation-regulations-sor-96-433/standards/standard-501-annual-airworthiness-information-report-canadian-aviation-regulations-cars) |
| `T` PROP | **AAIR: all applicable PROP** (fixed or variable pitch) · **separate PROP tech record: variable-pitch only** [CAR 605.92](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.92.html). |
| `T` | **AAIR ≠ annual INSP**; AAIR notice not received ≠ exemption; out-of-service declaration requires qualified reporting + return notification. |

`M:` **AAIR: owner→TC ≤MAR30 after JAN–DEC (or pre-agreed date) | CAWIS/24-0059 | air time+INSP/AME/AMO exceptions+DMG+ACFT/ENG/PROP data | O/S reported→no AAIR until RTS notice | AAIR PROP any type ≠ tech-record variable-pitch PROP only**.

#### Journey log / technical records — CAR 605.92–.97
<!-- id: law-journey-log-technical-records-car-605-92-97 | from: 04 | level: PPL CPL -->

| CAVE | Rule / trap |
|---|---|
| `R` records | Owner: journey log + airframe + **each ENG** + **each variable-pitch PROP** technical record + applicable components + empty W&B report; allowed combining cases apply. [605.92](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.92.html) |
| `T` fixed PROP | **No separate fixed-pitch PROP technical record required**; applicable MAINT still recorded. |
| `R` journey | Air time/defects/MAINT/releases entered per prescribed schedules/timing; PIC checks defects + MAINT status. [605.94](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.94.html) |
| `R` carry | Journey log onboard **unless CAR 605.95 exception** (e.g. no planned LDG + engine shutdown outside DEP). [605.95](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.95.html) |
| `R` retain | Technical-record retention varies by record/STD 625; **no universal period**. [605.96](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.96.html) |
| `R` title transfer | Transfer **all related technical records with aeronautical product at transfer**. [605.97](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-605.97.html) |

`M:` **JL + airframe + each ENG + variable-pitch PROP only + W&B | JL onboard unless exception | title→records | MAINT entry≠release**.

#### Unserviceable equipment — no MEL
<!-- id: law-unserviceable-equipment-no-mel | from: 04 | also: law-equip | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- If equipment is required by applicable day/night VFR/IFR standard, manufacturer equipment list, certificate, AD or CAR: no takeoff with it unserviceable/removed. `[CAR 605.10]`
- Non-required defective equipment may permit flight only if isolated/secured, placarded, and recorded as applicable. `[CAR 605.10]`
- PIC cannot fly if safety is affected regardless of deferral mechanics. `[CAR 605.08]`
- `M:` `REQUIRED? → SERVICEABLE? → DEFER LEGALLY? → SAFE?`
***
#### Synthetic flight training equipment — 606.03
<!-- id: law-synthetic-training-equipment-606-03 | level: CPL -->

- `R` Training or skills assessment under Part IV, VI or VII on a simulator/FTD only with a **flight simulator** or **flight training device certificate** (or recognized foreign equivalent). Not RPAS.
- `R` Certificate shows operator, ACFT type/model represented, qualification level, issue date. Stays valid only if performance kept (except per SCIG), maintained per the Simulator Manual, updated for ADs/rule changes affecting training.
- `R` Re-evaluation: **simulator every 6 months** (valid to 1st day of **7th** month) · **FTD every 12 months** (to 1st day of **13th** month). Minister may extend **≤60 days**.

- Sources: [606.03](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/section-606.03.html).

#### Pebble
<!-- id: law-airworthiness-documents-maintenance-pebble | level: PPL CPL IR -->

- DOCS `CofR on board | flight authority in effect, normally carried | AFM/POH if required | journey log unless 605.95 exception | radio licence if issued | insurance required (606.02), not a universal carry rule | valid document ≠ airworthy aircraft`
- REG `CoR ≠ proof of title | MTOW not printed on CoR | transfer → CoR cancelled, TC in writing ≤7 days | interim ≤3 mo, no second successive | temporary ≤3 mo | provisional route-specific | CoA independent of CoR`
- FLIGHT AUTHORITY `C of A standard | Special C of A: provisional / restricted / amateur-built / limited / owner-maintenance | Flight Permit: experimental / specific purpose | amateur-built ≠ experimental in Canada`
- MAINT `AME = licensed individual | AMO = approved organization, not required for every private inspection | AMO → PRM | AOC → PRMCS | MPM AMO / MCM operator | DML ≠ MEL | defect entry ≠ maintenance release | abnormal occurrence → inspection before return to service`
- RECORDS `journey log + airframe + each ENG + each variable-pitch PROP + W&B | no separate fixed-pitch PROP record | title transfer → all records`
- AAIR `owner → TC ≤MAR 30 after JAN–DEC period, or pre-agreed date | AAIR ≠ annual inspection`
- UNSERVICEABLE `REQUIRED? → SERVICEABLE? → DEFER LEGALLY? → SAFE? | required equipment unserviceable → no takeoff (605.10)`
- SIM/FTD `certificate required | sim re-eval 6 mo (7th mo) | FTD 12 mo (13th mo) | +60 d max`
